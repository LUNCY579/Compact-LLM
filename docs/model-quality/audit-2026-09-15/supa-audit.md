## Main decisions

1. The strongest **SupaExperiment-specific ordinary sidecar candidate** is CAPABILITY-1's existing `matched_mlp` bundle on exact Supa-A1.1, not the one-parameter gate-only bundle. It is development evidence from one deterministic training seed, with transfer instruments, not confirmed broad efficacy. It cannot be ranked against Opallo/B28last20 without a shared evaluation.
2. Existing Supa CALIB-1/2/3/4 and CAPABILITY-1 are **already executed**. Do not launch them as unexecuted repair work. The separate ChatBubble registered behavior repair must be assessed in ChatBubble; it is not interchangeable with this series.
3. MLP-SHIP-001 is the live additive qualification lane for the existing sidecar. It has 142 CPU fixture tests, zero pretrained calls, native BLOCKED_PREMODEL, and **no implemented M2 task-bank collector**. Historical shipping recommendation does not mean shipping qualification or actual deployment.
4. WSD-001 is a fresh matched-MLP schedule comparison, not dense B28last20, and **has never demonstrated a native WSD quality result**. WSD-NQ-001 supplies a zero-update harness but its committed attempt was blocked before the first forward.
5. The corrected symmetric generator is the most useful reusable contribution for a new MQ training bank. The frozen 2,400-row bank is an evaluated development instrument; it is not an untouched training/confirmation corpus. Do not relabel its outcomes or simply use a new seed as proof of template/family independence.
6. FLOOR-1 overturns the blanket claim that frozen Supa can never exceed query-blind state-tracking heuristics. An exemplar prompt clears the corrected d2 and marginal d3 floors, while wrecking retrieval and abstention. This supports objective/data work to combine capabilities, not immediate recurrence or exemplar deployment.
7. Hybrid memory is a completed registered negative promotion result, even though its remote branch is now deleted. Preserve its local historical branch and full recovery evidence. Do not call it merely pending or inert; the memory path was expressed but did not establish a stable benefit over the matched stateless control.
