## Owner-directed preservation rule

The owner explicitly requested a dedicated, durable, complementary research
track. Do not delete, archive, abandon, label obsolete, or replace CRS-001 merely
because a newer paper, model, architecture, or branch appears; because another
track improves; because resources are temporarily unavailable; or because one
pilot is negative. Do not discard existing discoveries in favor of CRS-001.

Retirement, archival, or replacement of the track requires explicit owner
approval and the provenance/dependency handoff specified in the charter. A
negative result may reject a hypothesis or procedure without deleting the track
or its evidence. Parking the work must retain its open questions and restart
conditions. These are repository workflow instructions, not an assertion that
GitHub branch protection has been configured.

## Scope and evidence

The founding change is documentation and a descriptive manifest only. It grants
no new native-run, training, paid-service, model-upload, merge, or deletion
permissions. Keep existing CHECK/FINAL banks and launch gates closed. Do not
lower resource or numerical checks to accommodate this proposal.

Keep future CRS-001 implementation isolated under
`experiments/rineya_centralized_reasoning_system_v1/` unless a separately
reviewed integration explicitly expands that scope. Preserve source branches,
weights, prior receipts, and the central program's original objective.

Distinguish supplied code from discovered procedures, CPU fixtures from native
model results, and system-level gains from model-only or distilled gains.
Refresh current source and host evidence before any future launch; the founding
snapshot is not live qualification.
