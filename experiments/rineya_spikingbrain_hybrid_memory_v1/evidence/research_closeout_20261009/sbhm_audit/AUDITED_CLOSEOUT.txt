7. Evidence boundary and next concrete decision

H1 (added distant evidence benefit above C): unsupported in this exact pilot.
H2 (bounded converted-site state): supported by code/geometry and saved native
snapshots at registered shapes. It does not imply total-model memory savings
beyond the measured small fraction. H3 (broad original capability preservation):
unestablished; narrow source-correct synthetic retention has documented damage.
H4 (optimized cost benefit): untested, with a slower sequential reference observed.

One source/site/seed 17, synthetic closed worlds, repeated DEV inspection, exact
string contracts and dependent world variants limit the result. Other 27 layers
retain full attention. No state-reset causal contrast or exclusive recurrent route
was measured. Artifact consistency cannot independently prove historical execution
or cryptographically prove operator consent. No confidence interval, promotion
threshold, general recurrence rejection, broad retention or energy claim is made.

Close the current evidence package with its negative result intact and hold all
additional SBHM native spending. There is no specific new mechanism plus minimal
discriminating control supported strongly enough here to justify a successor.
The evidence does not select among alternative explanations such as site choice,
forgetting dynamics, limited training geometry or remaining full-attention routes.
Do not automatically expand sites/context/recovery or launch a recurrence campaign.

Reopening requires a written new mechanistic hypothesis, the smallest control
that can discriminate it, prospectively fixed pairing/retention analysis and
resource bounds, one exact reviewed run proposal, and separate native permission.
The next concrete decision is to retain this justified hold; no execution command,
new grant, promotion or external spend is prepared by this closeout.
