# Rineya update-order quality follow-up

Prospective question: does balanced update-record ordering improve greatest-revision
selection over biased repeated ordering, without newly damaging source-correct
synthetic retention? This is a quality experiment with unchanged full attention.
The completed SBHM-001 negative recurrence result and all its receipts remain intact.

The [2026-10-09 publication archive](../evidence/research_closeout_20261009/README.md)
records the reviewed implementation and the later partial native attempt: three
qualifications and the anchor measurement completed, then control training stopped
at the unchanged runtime RAM guard. No completed adaptation comparison or ordering
gain exists. Historical proposals remain evidence; publication grants no execution.

Implementation and offline corpus approval are **not native-run authorization**.
Default commands never load pretrained weights. No historical grant, trained B/C/D
delta, teacher capture, recurrence, attention distillation, serving change, model
promotion, protected bank or automatic follow-up belongs to this study.

## Fixed design

Anchor: exact frozen Titan-Transition 1.0.0-devfreeze. Control/candidate: fresh
full-attention LoRA at layer 14, rank 8, seed 17, FP32, AdamW (.9,.95), learning
rate 0.0001, eps 1e-8, no weight decay, clip 1, mean answer/EOS causal loss.
Each trained condition gets 512 optimizer updates with one shared exposure-slot order.

384 update slots represent 16 worlds, two factual counterfactuals, two evidence
positions, six exposures. Control repeats one order; highest revision appears
first/middle/last in 12/2/2 worlds. Candidate enumerates all six permutations.
The other 128 slots are identical: 40 general evidence, 24 present/absent, and
64 retention examples. Matched slots have identical facts, targets, input lengths
and label counts; input token sequences differ by the registered intervention.
Update slots are 75% of optimizer steps, not necessarily 75% of tokens or labels.

Fresh common DEV: 672 rows (384 updates, 64 answerability, 160 general evidence,
64 retention). Six-order groups are dependent within 16 update worlds. Dataset
seed 170105; TRAIN/DEV worlds/templates and historical prompts remain separate.
TRAIN inputs are capped at 259 tokens; DEV prompts at 1158. The 2048-token native
qualification is a mechanics check, not empirical worst-shape training fit.

## Native boundary

The exact proposal has five variant qualifications, two 512-update adaptations,
three 672-row DEV measurements and one comparison. `run` without `--execute`
prints the proposal. Execution also needs `--allow-model-load`, `--allow-training`,
a new dated study/condition/action-bound native grant, and fresh ownership checks.
No grant is generated by preparation. A local grant is a self-recorded operator
acknowledgment, not cryptographic proof of consent.

The proposal's `grant_requirements` lists the exact grant schema and bindings.
Use its canonical `proposal_sha256`, not the raw proposal-file checksum (separately
recorded as `proposal_file_sha256` in preparation). The named operator must supply
timezone-aware issuance/expiry, maximum seven-day lifetime, and precisely the
listed condition/action set. A standalone stage requires an ownership
acknowledgment within five minutes. An uninterrupted authorized `run` holds an
exclusive orchestrator lock and performs current competing-worker scans at every
stage, preserving the original acknowledgment and immutable fresh scan receipts.
Use the pinned CUDA interpreter in a fresh process without tokenizer-only backend
disable flags for separately authorized execution.

Guards remain 7.4 GiB free RAM at admission, 6 GiB at runtime, 4 GiB worker RSS,
CUDA:0 without fallback, and 1 GiB free VRAM reserve. Every output delta needs
fresh exact-variant qualification. Any guard, hash, exposure, qualification or
stage failure stops the run. No automatic resume, retry, widening or seed search.

Before either adaptation, frozen-anchor retention must have at least 16 correct
cases spanning four skills **separately for strict and typed-value correctness**.
An inadequate instrument stops the study without changing the panel.

## Registered decision

On 256 greatest-not-first rows, candidate must add at least 26 net typed-correct
cases over control (at least 10 percentage points). Middle and last strata must
both improve, and more six-order groups must be correct in all six permutations.
Greatest-first accuracy and JSON contract compliance must not decline.

Candidate must damage zero anchor-correct retention cases, with strict and typed
denominators kept separate. Present and absent scores must each not decline versus
control. Report their absolute results even if both conditions remain poor.
Preserve damaged source-correct cases separately from repaired source-incorrect
ones, and distinguish formatting repairs from changed answer values.

Passing means `ADVANCE_TO_REVIEW_ONLY`; failing means `HOLD`. Neither is promotion
or broad capability confirmation. Scores measure the registered JSON contract;
there is no permissive answer salvage or model judge. Stable wrong answers fail.
