## Review findings and corrections

Two independent reviews checked the sampler mathematics, executable draws,
test oracles, qualifier, evidence, and interpretation boundaries. The recovered
28-check suite passed, and no transition-math defect was found. Review identified
two validation gaps:

1. The qualifier's `testsRun > 12` criterion could certify mechanics with an
   incomplete sampler suite. Unrelated green checks could replace missing EOS,
   restart, swap, or negative-control checks. Qualification now requires all
   named registration, sampler, and qualification-control tests to be both loaded
   and executed. It rejects skips, expected failures, failures/errors, and an
   execution count inconsistent with its recorded inventory. Extra tests are
   allowed and do not replace required checks. The new regression controls
   remove each required check in turn, substitute an unrelated successful check,
   and verify rejection despite an unchanged total count.
2. The original execution tests exercised implicit restart selection only for
   EOS/PAD. A review-only mutation forced default restart 0 for A-prefix states,
   consuming the original uniform RNG ticket before overriding it. All original
   16 sampler tests passed that mutation, although its stationary distribution
   was wrong: at power 1 the incoming-minus-target mass for `AA` was `-1/40`.
   The added execution test forces both implicit restart positions for every
   state and power, verifies all event fields and three integer draws, and
   rejects the same mutation with 27 failing subtests. The retained
   [mutation receipt](default_restart_mutation_2026-10-09.json) includes the
   exact mutation, source identities, and both suite outcomes. The production sampler
   already selected uniform restarts correctly and required no change.

These findings concern validation coverage. They do not establish a native
sampler defect, a reasoning gain, or evidence against interacting search.
