wall time, a native-terminal cache flag and `recovery_owner`, but omits caller and

unknown/incomplete cost dispositions. Failures before or after TC therefore have
no inspectable complete cost result. Known measurements are trapped in mutable
engine state rather than included in failure evidence. In `main` (1633-1637) the
owner is removed for the console sink, so even that incidental route disappears.

costs may remain unknown, and K's own late durability work need not be universally
abortable. The accepted scope nevertheless requires recording all failed costs
and measuring K without an exemption. A genuinely measured11s U CPU excess can
cause refusal while the returned failure record contains no CPU amount at all.
An unknown post-stop sample must be labeled unknown and preserve the prior valid
measurements; silence or an opaque owner is not that producer.

The planned `rejected` helper and CPU/resource/late-publication controls assert
failure status, no retry and selected reservation/native-state conditions. They
do not assert failed-phase CPU/cost production, preservation of known overruns,
explicit missing-measurement state or a bound to retained sample evidence. Thus
the planned suite would not reject this gap even if all34 groups passed.

Finite resolving criterion: make the integrated failure result bind the selected
phase, T0/TC/available endpoint times, known CPU origin/TC/last valid role samples,
