   refuse/terminate owned work, retain query/ownership witnesses and unknown

   necessarily completes at10s or drop a live owner to make the clock pass.
3. **Complete measured endpoint.** Define success only after actual owned exit,
   required EOF/resource evidence, durable caller receipt and complete terminal
   serialization/publication. The observer measures these events after they
   happen, with immutable source/request/runtime/worker bindings and original
   bytes/hashes. A pre-write clock sample, version-output availability or worker
   interval is not caller completion. Numeric handle snapshots are not live
   ownership or durable transfer proof.
4. **Finite observer/accounting interval.** Declare the observer's validation,
   evidence publication and canonical reconciliation interval and its clock/cost

5. **Canonical outcome discipline.** Bind the exact canonical store/lease/fence/
   epoch/request/reservation and recorded worker. No terminal SUCCEEDED from

   outcome/cost is unproved; charge actual measured qualification/failure/overrun
   according to the unchanged policy, never cap a known overrun or reset history.
   Existing immutable worker-exit receipts must not be silently replaced or
   misrepresented as complete caller observations.
