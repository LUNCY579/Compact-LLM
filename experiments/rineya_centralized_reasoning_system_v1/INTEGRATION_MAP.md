# Complementarity and integration boundaries

CRS-001 is a shared reasoning-runtime research track, not a declaration that
existing Rineya work should be consolidated away. The [charter](../../RINEYA_CENTRALIZED_REASONING_SYSTEM.md)
requires explicit owner approval for retirement or archival of this track and
preserves the independent value of earlier discoveries.

## Three different research objects

1. **Native model:** what a particular checkpoint can do without the proposed
   external procedure library and executor.
2. **Centralized reasoning system:** what that checkpoint plus disclosed
   retrieval, state, tools, procedures, and verification can do.
3. **Central research program:** how native execution, acquisition, retention,
   evidence, and experimental authorization are governed.

Sharing a repository or the word "centralized" does not make these the same
object. CRS-001's parent is the central program so its provenance and safeguards
remain available, not so that native gates are inherited as passed.

## Connections to existing directions

The entries below describe intended interfaces, not newly audited efficacy
claims or permission to merge or activate another experiment.

| Existing direction | How it could complement CRS-001 | What must not be inferred |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Central Titan post-training and retention | Supplies a qualified checkpoint, native execution contracts, and later controlled distillation/retention evaluation. | External-system gains do not satisfy the central native acquisition or retention objective. |
| Retrieval and long-context diagnostics | Supplies evidence-selection baselines and methods to separate reading, presentation, and reasoning errors. | Prior selector failure does not prove retrieval universally useless; successful lookup does not prove reasoning. |
| Sparse updates, functional composition, model soup, and skill retention | Could improve individual reader/planner roles or internalize useful operations. | Parameter orthogonality does not guarantee compatible procedures or lossless behavioral composition. |
| Attention, KV-cache, and factorized-memory work | Could reduce per-call resource costs or improve retained neural evidence. | An external procedure store is not a KV cache; one does not obsolete the other. |
| Recurrence, looped refinement, and CED/cross-scale architectures | Could provide stronger bounded-context workers behind the same typed interface. | Tool loops are not native recurrence; a runtime result does not qualify a changed architecture. |
| Abstention and uncertainty research | Could improve missing-evidence detection and stopping, tested on separate answerability strata. | More refusals or a higher total score alone is not better reasoning. |
| Verified resampling and self-distillation | Could use independently validated development traces after their own gates pass. | Self-agreement is not truth; test answers cannot become training material. |
| Dream-RSI and experiment-controller work | Could help organize development experiments and preserve receipts under explicit permissions. | A research controller is not automatically a qualified question-answering controller or a self-improvement result. |

## Safe future integration

Integrate one component at a time, bind exact source/model/artifact identities,
and compare against an otherwise identical system. A merge or interface adapter
is not qualification. Do not modify existing models, weights, objectives,
launch flags, retention banks, or historical receipts to make CRS-001 easier to
report. Do not assign family roles based only on model names.

Preserve dedicated source branches and attach evidence to the exact experiment
that generated it. New work should report whether it is a component test,
system evaluation, model-only study, or distillation study. Update this map with
reviewed source pointers when actual integrations are proposed; do not silently
promote conceptual connections into completed integrations.
