# PARENTQ and Titan quality research evidence

Published on 2026-10-09 from branch `research/triskill-geo001-parentq`.
The preparation, measurements and discoveries in these records date to 2026-10-05.
This archive publishes source preparation and CPU evidence. It does not authorize
a native run, qualify a parent, establish Rineya capability gains, adopt a style
map, change serving policy or promote a checkpoint. Geometric composition remains
deferred. The model-quality research goal remains blocked pending separate native
hardware-slot authorization and prospective data decisions.

The [archive manifest](ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.json) maps 273 byte-exact copies to their
original external paths, byte counts and SHA256 digests. Originals remain under
BusinessProject. Receipt contents and embedded absolute paths are preserved for
historical integrity; use the relative links here when reading the Git archive.
An embedded path can be resolved by finding its `source_path` in the manifest.

Historical documents describe HEAD `3e07fd8740e8ff4debd94f9c17ca3e06d056a0ed`
and then-untracked files. Those statements describe the preparation snapshot,
not the publication commit. Likewise, the diagnostics source README's "pending
row audit" statement predates the completed `cases-002` receipt. Existing guarded
package bytes were preserved so their source-bound evidence remains auditable.

## Included and deliberately external evidence

The archive includes small JSON/Markdown/log records, failed attempts, discovery
summaries, source snapshots for historical hashes and the scoped review records.
It excludes raw training/DEV JSONL payloads, the quarantined prospective final
archives, checkpoint/tokenizer binaries, environments, caches and fixture SQLite
state. Their manifests retain identities where applicable. No sealed bank payload
is copied or opened for publication. Portable source and receipts cannot replace
unperformed native measurements or a genuine operator grant.
