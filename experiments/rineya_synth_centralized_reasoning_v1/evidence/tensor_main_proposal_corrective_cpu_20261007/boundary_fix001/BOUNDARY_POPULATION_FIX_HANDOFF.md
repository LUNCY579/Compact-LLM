final and returned `selected_boundary_counters` were absent. The matched
producer always supplies a dictionary. The consumer now requires dictionaries
on both records, including an explicitly empty population. Early provisional
reports also require their corresponding final maps, rather than defaulting an
omitted map to empty.

with CPU schema metadata. The new negative removes the boundary population from

reseals original/preserved raw descriptors; the consumer refuses it. The five
existing composition controls still pass. SUP/Job/resources/death remain
invented CPU fixture records, and the nonfixture shared native gate stays closed.
