# Accepted retention-source coverage still incomplete

The accepted [supplemental quality policy](evidence/supplemental_quality_packet_reviewed_20261005/QUALITY_SCREEN_POLICY_001.json)
requires arithmetic, retrieval, abstention and dialogue source-correct retention,
plus separate genuine long-context qualification/enrollment. Its decision
consumer refuses missing source-correct support; zero support is undefined.
Keep that refusal and the historical precision/margin rules unchanged.

The [task guard policy](TASK_RETENTION_GUARD_POLICY.json) plans retrieval,
instruction-following and answerability/abstention candidate mechanisms.
Instruction-following cannot satisfy arithmetic coverage. These candidates
remain unenrolled and unqualified; naming a mechanism is not actual M0-correct
membership. Arithmetic requires a separately frozen population and qualified
scorer with disjoint source-correct enrollment before treatment outcomes.

[Language handoff](LANGUAGE_GATE_HANDOFF.md) records authored candidate worlds,
zero actual qualified ratings/reference vectors and no qualified graders.
Authored language candidates do not establish qualified dialogue. Dialogue
population/scorer/calibration/authority and sufficient independent source-correct
support remain open. Separate long-context qualification/enrollment is also
missing. Do not substitute short guards or literal/JSON checks for these domains.

The staged production compositions require task/language/long-context domain
classes and exact declared scorer/enrollment joins. Those class-level joins do
not by themselves prove all four accepted retention-source mechanisms. Future
reviewed comparison packets must bind both required classes and exact accepted
source populations, scorers, qualification and source-correct membership.

Full384 M0 raw capture/scoring is incomplete, and M1/M2 have not produced a
transfer or retention result. This note preserves the accepted coverage gap;
it changes no training dose, cohort, margin, resource limit or domain definition,
and grants no native/scoring authority. A structural coverage registry may
track missing descriptors but cannot qualify or enroll them.
