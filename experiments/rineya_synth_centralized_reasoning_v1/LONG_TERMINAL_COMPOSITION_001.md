# Whole terminal-frame composition; native certification disabled

`long_terminal_driver.py` composes the independently pinned long validators over
supplied JSON and byte fixtures. It checks selected config/result/supervisor/
inventory bytes, raw/source preservation, state/parity/RNG observations,
physical prefixes/dose, publication costs, cross-role lineage and owned resource/
lease records together. Positive fixture outputs retain a CPU-only kind.

The native entrypoint rejects before output or large I/O. Actual filesystem and
current process-identity orchestration, exact V2 registration consumption and
full scope reconstruction remain unimplemented. Mutually consistent self-sealed
scope/inventory maps do not authenticate themselves. A complete-runtime boolean
or a prepared package/stdlib candidate cannot provide final native authority.
The driver cannot certify a run, authorize resume, enroll guards or score answers.

All [12 root controls](evidence/long_terminal_composition_root_cpu_validation_20261005.json)
pass from reviewed bytes. They include substituted raw/source bytes, omitted
maps, stale or forged lineage, incorrect state/parity/physical metadata, partial
costs, failed cleanup, GPU/RSS/identity/lease failures and native entry denial.
Stage and fresh-root tests use explicit driver paths; generic module-name
collisions and ignored-stage dependencies are avoided. The
[17-file history](evidence/long_terminal_composition_reviewed_cpu_20261005)
retains the owner-fixture failure, V1 preview rejection and portability repair.

No model, framework, encoder, GPU, OS probe or large binary was executed by this
CPU milestone. RNG evidence remains hashes, without raw-state restoration;
resident tensors are not reread by the driver. The exact additive V2 contract,
actual native terminal driver, current model fit and later study comparison
still require evidence. Transfer and prior-correct retention remain unmeasured.
