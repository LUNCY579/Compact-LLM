# Language preservation requirements still open

The central program's public language proposal and authority audit report no
qualified reference vectors, assigned graders or authored DEV panel. Its rubric
requires separate adequacy, relevance, fluency and role-consistency judgments;
literal correctness or JSON validity cannot establish fluency. Independent
reference/control validity, identified reviewers, calibration, blinding,
adjudication and complete cost limits must be established before certification.
Managed-agent annotations alone cannot provide that authority.

SYNTH's independent [precision check](evidence/language_precision_handoff_20261004.json)
also shows that the central proposal's 64 independent DEV worlds would be
insufficient for SYNTH's registered five-percentage-point retention bound. With
three declared guard domains and two candidate arms, the comparison alpha is
0.05/6. Even zero losses among 64 M0-correct components gives a 7.2075% upper
bound. At least 94 eligible independent components are required for a bound
at or below 5%; more declared domains or fewer M0-correct worlds change that
requirement. CAL controls and paraphrase/counterfactual siblings cannot increase
the independent DEV denominator.

A sufficient fresh language population and its assignments must be registered
before candidate outputs, with all worlds retained. Do not weaken the margin,
merge domains after seeing outcomes, or resample to replace M0-incorrect rows.
Insufficient enrollment precision remains inconclusive even with zero losses.
This handoff does not change central proposals, authorize paid graders, open
protected banks or establish language qualification. The pending request for
existing qualified evidence or independent reviewer identities remains open.
