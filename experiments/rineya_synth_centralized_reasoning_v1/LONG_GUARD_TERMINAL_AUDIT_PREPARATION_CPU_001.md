The staged consumer joins the existing decoded-long worker report to saved batch,
token partials, final rows, enriched Journal, physical meter and terminal records.
It reuses the original collector's raw-prefix validation and original event and
resource validators. Runtime parity, decode, postcheck and hook-cleanup operations
are accounted separately from the core's forward-only costs.

The physical join requires two 419-token parity calls plus the actual capture
forwards and positions, within the unchanged 2,050-forward total. It retains raw
capture packet hashes, canonical source indices and exact row/token filenames.
Canonical comparisons distinguish Boolean and numeric fields. Generic raw-file
reads are bounded and checked for regular paths before hashing; JSON and journal
inputs remain nonempty. Final selected-byte and raw/lease roster checks follow
fresh observations of every recorded launcher/interpreter birth.
