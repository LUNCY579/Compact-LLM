# Baseline-first measurement ordering

The [CPU amendment](BASELINE_FIRST_MEASUREMENT_AMENDMENT_001.json) separates
qualified M0-only scoring and source-correct enrollment from treatment imports
and comparison. It removes the circular requirement that future M1/M2 endpoints
exist before M0 can be scored. Complete qualified M0 scores and disjoint
source-correct guard membership must be frozen before the first treatment update.

The original [finish policy](DEV_FINISH_POLICY_001.json) remains byte-for-byte
preserved. Its EOS/cap correctness rule, full384/96 denominator and honest
after192 timing disclosure are unchanged. Actual final27/540 M1/M2 endpoints,
three-chunk publication/cost lineage and all three identities remain prerequisites
for treatment import and comparison. Future checkpoint hashes are bound after
production to the selection rule fixed before training.

This adopts reviewed CPU metadata under the original user criterion of transfer
while preserving prior-correct behavior and the existing baseline-before-candidate
enrollment contract. It asserts no unverified stronger human chronology quotation.
The [external review draft](evidence/baseline_first_measurement_reviewed_cpu_20261006/EXTERNAL_REVIEW_DRAFT_002.json)
and [independent review](evidence/baseline_first_measurement_reviewed_cpu_20261006/EXTERNAL_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_002.json)
are preserved inputs, not permission. Native scoring, training and launch remain
closed; the existing importer is unchanged and still cannot grade native records.

Historical5pp-plus-CI and supplemental3pp engineering policies retain their separate
identities. Guard scorers remain domain-specific; R/S scoring cannot qualify
dialogue, language or long-context retention. Missing or zero source-correct
support remains undefined. No response, oracle, model or tokenizer was read, and
no scorer or native action ran. Actual transfer and preservation remain unmeasured.
