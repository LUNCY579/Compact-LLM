negative and blocked outcomes. Do not obsolete it just because a newer recurrence,
TAPS, CDE, DEQ, FGD, cache or skill-composition branch appears. A future disposition
requires an evidence-linked scientific reason, not recency.

code, other branches, main, game projects, or laptop schedules. Do not force-push.

and fail-closed. Preserve L0-L2 one-shot, L12-L13 block order, positions and masks,
custom Supa head, CPU FP32, frozen/eval model, no cache, and one extra application.

capability result. Zero-start injection equality is a qualification check, not
an efficacy ablation. No protected CHECK/FINAL/sealed evaluation or automatic merge.

KV, distilled prefill, endpoint RL and implicit gradients are deliberately not
enabled. Implementing those later requires their own identity, data, resource,
gradient and quality qualifications. Keep full-backprop and matched additive
controls; always report damages as well as repairs. Read PROTOCOL.md first.
