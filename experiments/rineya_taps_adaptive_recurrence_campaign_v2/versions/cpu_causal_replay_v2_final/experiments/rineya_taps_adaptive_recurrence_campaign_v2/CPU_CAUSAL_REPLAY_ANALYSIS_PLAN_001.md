## Causal and predictor firewall

Each answer target j must match the frozen original input plus canonical tokens
before j. EOS follows the full canonical answer; accepted set is151643/151645.
Check sequence position/hash/length, not token-value membership. Current/future
positions never enter the target's prefix; earlier gold legitimately does.

Predictor B comes exclusively from features.jsonl's original INPUT_ONLY unit
trajectory: original-prefix hash, zero preceding-gold count, target-independent,
frozen before loss, and canonical freeze hash. It must match answer-position0's
raw balance. Teacher-prefix balances remain diagnostic-only. Loss, sensitivity,
gradient norm or teacher provenance cannot be predictor fields, even at the first
input prefix. No fallback to a later target or label-dependent feature is allowed.

## Endpoints and numerical uncertainty

Recompute primary per-case mean answer CE at each fixed alpha and its mean
adjoint from all answer-target rows; compare to saved case outcomes. Recompute
the mean FD as `(mean_plus-mean_minus)/.02`. Both every target and the case mean
must pass `abs(adjoint-FD)<=.001+.03*abs(adjoint)`; cancellation does not widen
tolerance. Answer sums/summed derivatives are separate descriptive canonical
answer PREFIX likelihood: a longer wrong response may share the full prefix.
Spelling/whitespace/case equivalence and on-policy behavior are not assessed.

All192 mean values remain in the report. Neutral is abs(mean_adjoint)<=.001.
A nonneutral case is NUMERICALLY_UNCERTAIN when adjoint and mean FD differ in
sign, including FDzero. Reliable sign comparisons exclude only those declared
neutral/uncertain cases and report each denominator and every case flag. Neither
uncertainty nor neutrality excuses a failed gate or deletes raw observations.

On reliable cases, compare sign(B) with the local loss-decreasing direction
(-sign(adjoint)), and with prespecified constant increase/decrease. Report actual
mean-loss deltas at.99/1.01 independently: a point derivative does not guarantee
finite-step improvement. Tied-average-rank Spearman(B,-mean_adjoint) over all192
numerically qualified cases is descriptive, with no p-value, fit or broad claim.

EOS is secondary: accepted-stop probability loss and derivative are reconstructed
separately. Canonical EOT151645 CE is descriptive only; it has no extra adjoint.
No answer+EOS primary composite, favorable endpoint selection or hiding EOS harm.
EOS after supplied canonical text does not establish generated termination.

## Related variants, strata and limits

Average variant mean-loss/derivative values within each parent and parents within
category for population summaries. Report per-case/variant loss changes and any
opposite within-world directions even when parent averages cancel. Directional
case counts and equal-parent conditional scores have explicit denominators;
no target/prefix/adjoint repetition creates independent evidence. Keep all96
parent rows and category/difficulty/answerability/source-status summaries.

First five categories' same-gold pairs and category6 evidence-transfer pairs have
different meanings. Report same-world coherence/direction consistency and source
responsiveness descriptively; do not interpret different gold histories as a
matched generation intervention. Shared six generators/authors and exposed DEV
permit no independent source confirmation, equivalence or automatic advancement.
'Survive strata' is a descriptive audit, not a trained or selected rule.

Incomplete source/mechanics/matrix evidence yields no primary mechanism result.
Complete proxy associations cannot fill failed bulk validation, prove semantic
preservation, justify weight training or a controller fit, resolve the GPU fault,
or establish deployable speed. The analyzer records its own source and input
hashes; any later correction requires a versioned result/receipt, never rewriting
frozen raw evidence or selecting a convenient subset.
