## Question and population

Can ordinary-pass, last-token movement or intact-head confidence distinguish
cases where recurrence repairs an answer from cases where it destroys one?
Record both low-confidence and high-confidence interpretations rather than
assuming that greater confidence or larger movement indicates task progress.

Use all 192 original DEVELOPMENT prompts from 96 related parents, with the
existing source-bound token IDs. Each receives an A0 and unit-K1 first-prefix
full forward. There is no generation, teacher prefix, label-conditioned
forward, state adjoint, fitted gate or online action. The prompt-only export
contains no gold, score, answerability, source-status, category or parent labels.
Case IDs are logging/join keys only. Historical generation outcomes and
parent/family metadata may be joined offline after the native run terminates.

## Analysis and advancement

Describe all features, zero/nonfinite rows, A0/unit consistency, full population
and parent/family coverage. Join frozen historical outcomes after termination
to distinguish unit-only, A0-only, both-correct and both-wrong groups. Treat
local derivative and generated quality labels separately. Report overlap and
direction reversals, including ordinary cue-only explanations; do not drop
ties, cap cases or inconvenient strata. No threshold is fitted in this study.

A promising association would justify a separately registered deterministic
policy, opposed orientation, cue-only/static and marginal-matched random K
controls, actual full generations and authorized unexposed parent/source
replication. Model scores must stay beside own-bank shortcuts and strong
ordinary/stateless controls. Keep the original practical-margin and preservation
limits explicit; this study's small ceiling does not promise a five-point gain.
