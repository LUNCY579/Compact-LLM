## Exact integration

Use new modules rather than change `gpu_native.evaluate_case`, `control.BlockRecurrence`, observer, full-matrix worker or old guards. A proposed raw generation adapter imports the qualified `gpu_native.forward_logits`, original-prefix encoding, resource/conditioning/timing/anchor helpers and `control.BlockRecurrence`. It copies the inherited greedy decoding mechanics into a new label-free interface. The old evaluator unconditionally computes its own first logits and invokes scorers, so it cannot safely supply pilot reuse or the requested native label firewall through its current signature.

The online policy receives only its frozen name, raw prompt and, when required, actual intact-pilot probability. Never pass the case dictionary, IDs, category, parent, split, source status, scorer, gold, old response, derivative or teacher features. IDs remain outer journal joins. Prefer a raw generation worker with no correctness scorer or gold at all; after terminal proof, a separate offline reader joins the frozen labels/scorer. Policy certificates select K only and never supply or rewrite an answer. Guard parsing and certificate logging have measured costs.

For each physical confidence-policy evaluation, perform its own intact original-prefix pilot under K0. Finish and restore that wrapper before entering the selected generation context. Bind the live pilot handle to this run/model instance, resident state/source/tokenizer hashes, exact prefix IDs/hash, CUDA device, FP32dtype and actual forward-journal ID. It contains a cloned finite actual151936-row logit vector. Only the same-run factory may create it, and it may be consumed once. No caller-supplied or serialized logits, old probe readout, response replay, KV state or hidden-state cache may stand in for the pilot.

Compute the feature using exactly the frozen observer expression: `z=logits.detach().float(); logp=z.log_softmax(0); p_vector=logp.exp(); p=float(p_vector[z.argmax()])`. Do not reconstruct from recorded logsumexp, replace the kernel, smooth probabilities or filter head rows. A minimal online helper may omit other descriptive statistics only after exact parity to this expression is qualified. Require finite logits/probability in[0,1]. Freeze and journal p and the K decision before token1. CONFIDENT uses K1 for p>=0.5, UNCERTAIN for p<0.5; equality belongs to CONFIDENT. Two physically independent pilots must agree exactly and the decisions must complement if the paired17N cap is registered. A discrepancy stops the study; it does not trigger a tolerance, alternate threshold or retry. Probability is not correctness evidence.

When K0 is selected, start greedy decoding directly from the pilot's logits. No second first-prefix forward occurs. Then, for every needed continuation, append only that rollout's previously chosen token and run a fresh cache-free full prefix under a new K0 context. When K1 is selected, preserve the untouched original IDs and make an additional unit full forward before choosing token1. Its logits—not the pilot logits—start the answer; every subsequent prefix uses only its own generated tokens under unit K1. K remains fixed for the whole answer. Do not generate an intact token before switching to unit or use another arm's continuation. The actual pilot logit vector can be released after decision/first-token consumption and its recorded operands/hash are committed.

Keep `TAPSConfig(scheduler_mode="unit")` and exact alpha-1 direct-proposal behavior. Constructor extra_loops is0or1; do not mutate it inside an active wrapper. The model prehook resets moments for every full forward, and context exit restores forwards/hooks/ownership. Full attention, all-one input mask, automatic positions, saved replay conditioning and no cache remain unchanged. L0–L2 run once per full call: a K1 pilot is an additional charged full call, not an additional in-call anchor application. No weights, head bias, operator, depth or precision changes occur.

The prompt guard/header cue and matched case-level random controls decide before their first model call and need no head pilot. Guard rejection means the draft's explicit K1 fallback, not partial parsing or an answer supplied by a solver. Matched random K is the draft's stdlib seed17/29, one draw per item in frozen order, frozen outcome-blind DEV marginal; K is held throughout generation. It is not token-level random alpha. If uniform pilot telemetry is later required for these controls, preregister and charge it; do not silently add or hide it.

A repeated logical first-token use is not another physical call or a zero-cost copied outcome.
