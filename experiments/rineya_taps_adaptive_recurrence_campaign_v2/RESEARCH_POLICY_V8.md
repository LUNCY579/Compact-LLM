# Policy 8: finalize bytes before source review and dispatch freeze

The CPU H3 cycle exposed a provenance failure: two documentation files were
formatted after the mathematics reviewer read them. The execution source and
scientific constraints were unchanged, but the original receipt no longer bound
the final documentation bytes. The coordinator found the mismatch before dispatch;
receipt 001 was preserved and a reviewed addendum bound the final files. No model
was started under the stale documentation lineage.

For the next independently authorized experiment, each owner declares source,
tests and documentation final before independent review. Keep those bytes fixed
through review, admission creation and dispatch. Include imported fixture helpers
and scientific registration dependencies in the freeze. If a change is necessary,
preserve the earlier receipt and obtain a correction or successor binding the
changed files before dispatch. Compare the complete binding graph, including
documentary reviews and addenda, at the final gate.

Acceptance evidence for this scoped procedure: preserved math receipt 001 and
addendum 002; final CPU source admission with 83 current bindings and 10 asset
identities; independent eight worker and six parent fixture checks; fresh readiness;
native CPU before/after source and loaded-state preservation. The exact CPU run
completed all registered work without a post-freeze source change. These are
provenance and execution acceptance checks, not a measured comparison of research
allocation policies or a claim of controller/agent improvement.

This policy changes neither authority, retry counts, protected data boundaries,
model/operator, scientific endpoints, fixed tolerances, resource floors, compute
limits nor outcome interpretation. A new experiment still needs its own reviewed
registration and qualification; no additional native job is authorized here.
