For uncertainty, report paired gains/losses and their scenario aggregation first. An optional exhaustive 2^13 cluster sign-flip diagnostic is computationally feasible, but its inferential assumptions are unestablished on this heterogeneous selected bank; label it descriptive and do not use it as confirmation. Bootstrap intervals cannot cure 13 or fewer dependent parent units or repeated DEV selection. If intact 12/16 and recurrent 13/16 reproduce, the maximum possible no-loss improvement gives exact unadjusted two-sided p=.125 or .25, respectively. This establishes limited discrimination before outcomes.

Do not perform confirmatory tests or claim population noninferiority on the reference screen. Retain every revision/contrast and its parent; predeclare a maximum six adaptive revisions and fixed acceptance/selection rules. A future independent, authorized bank must preregister its own parent/generator partitions, practical/preservation margins, primary fixed comparison and multiplicity policy before exposure. If the same bank later guides revisions, relabel it selection data.

The historical reference matrix forecast is 392 case evaluations and at most 6,348 generation forwards; shape metadata and qualified cost measurements must establish that the entire run fits the remaining 21,600-second budget with the campaign's reserves. Metadata preflight alone is insufficient. A separate corrected A5 comparison needs its own qualification/cost allocation and must not be silently substituted inside the immutable historical runner.
