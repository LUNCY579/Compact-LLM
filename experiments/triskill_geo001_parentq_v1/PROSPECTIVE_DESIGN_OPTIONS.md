no freeze is created, and no native execution is authorized.** This memo is one
component of the revised PARENTQ packet, not a separate executable protocol.

original meaning. Failed evidence is neither overwritten nor explained away.

The geometric hypothesis stays narrow: a checkpoint-derived intervention in the
parents' output subspaces, with fixed downstream coordinates, might improve
transition-conditioned answering/abstention. Arithmetic controls, an independent

that question. Layer 14, rank 4, eta=.1, the T/H deltas, and geometric composition
remain deferred. None of these options uses native J/K/TH/ROTATION outcomes.

## 2. Reliability of the parent gate

Exact measurement under A removes prediction error about a fixed deterministic
gate, but retains the gate's weak evidence standard. A pass is permission to
consider that particular exploratory screen only if separately authorized.
Four rows per parent cannot establish retention or a reliable general parent.
Repeated decoding of the same rows cannot increase the independent sample size.

The alternatives are to preserve the frozen gate as an explicitly mechanical,
exploratory feasibility check; strengthen it through a separate GEO amendment;
or leave the GEO screen deferred and run no native parent screen until a powered
parent instrument is approved. Any strengthened gate should preregister both
absolute correctness and differential signal, separate cue/distractor and
answerable performance, false abstention, unsupported answers, and damage to
BASE-correct rows. The [future protocol](../triskill_geometric_pilot_v1/FUTURE_PROTOCOL.md)
requires per-family retention and independently generated source worlds; its
planning floors are not power proofs for this parent instrument.

## 4. Q2, BASE ceilings and blanket abstention

Keep the frozen credited endpoint unchanged. Add strict, credited, semantic and
predeclared lenient answer counts on both H_ON and H_OFF, split answerable versus
cue/distractor. A secondary fixed natural-abstention vocabulary can describe
format failure without changing GEO's scoring or primary verdict. Pooling A/B
must be disclosed, or report them separately as correlated prompt variants of
the same 32 source worlds.

Before assigning a transport interpretation, choose between an evidence-vector
report with no categorical implication and a stronger preregistered selective
answerability diagnostic. For the latter, candidate floors are at least 10/12
answerable correct and at most 1/12 answerable false abstentions, together with
the missing-row paired contrast; alternatively use a powered noninferiority
requirement against BASE. These are prospective choices and these small counts
are not capability confirmation. Blanket abstention must receive an explicit
`OVER_ABSTENTION`/selectivity-failure flag even when its missing-row gain is
significant. A claim of specificity requires H-versus-BASE evidence under the
same prompt; absence of that evidence is not proof of non-specificity.

On exact screen measurement, BASE 4/4 is the existing
`STOP_SCREEN_PARENT_CEILING:<parent>` condition. On a generated bank, high or
perfect BASE accuracy means relative gain is unidentified or poorly measurable;
it does not establish that the fixed screen is at ceiling. Neither condition
supports "H does not transport." A/B improvement, a BASE ceiling, a null or
blanket abstention cannot automatically change the chains' abstention contract.
That would change J semantics and needs a separate prospective instrument
amendment. A delta_C study is a distinct research line requiring separate scope.

## 5. Canary, padding and every stop outcome

Choose the identity threshold before any native output: retain 7/8 with every
mismatch's first-divergence margin and source-match matrix; require 8/8 under a
strict exact-reproduction policy; or supplement the fixed text rule with pinned
weight/construction identity checks and a separately specified numerical parity
diagnostic. The review's stored-source separation is useful identity evidence,
but cannot justify lowering a threshold after a miss. Tiny top-1 margins can
explain a numerical difference descriptively; they cannot retroactively count
a wrong text as a pass.

| Prospective outcome | Required interpretation and next action |
|---|---|
| Padding failed | Native mechanics incompatible; no parent conclusion. Preserve tokens/margins and stop. Repair offline, review and re-freeze mechanical changes before a new grant. |
| Canary failed | Arm identity/numerical parity unresolved; no qualification from that arm. Preserve all partial data and diagnose offline. No post hoc canary replacement or threshold reduction. |
| Padding/canary never reached | Incomplete or blocked; report exactly which stage and whether LOAD_BEGUN exists. |
| Loaded attempt interrupted/crashed | Counts toward the cap even without a final verdict. Preserve progress and compare the next authorized overlap. |
| Exhausted loaded attempts | `ATTEMPTS_EXHAUSTED` with parents unmeasured/inconclusive; no implication about skill. No reset via a new directory or permit. |
| Overlap differs | `NONDETERMINISM_OBSERVED`; list token/text disagreements, stop automatic qualification and require a prospective rerun policy. Do not select the best attempt. |
| BASE ceiling | Differential parent signal unidentified; preserve the existing ceiling distinction. |
| Parent gate failed | Gate-specific failure under the selected population and endpoint; preserve both parents' component data. It does not reject geometry. |
| Parent gate passed | The selected pre-screen criterion passed; no third-skill evidence. Any GEO screen still needs its own reviewed freeze, ownership and grant. |

Loaded accounting is cumulative across all historical directories with a
create-only LOAD_BEGUN marker. A retry grant names attempt and freeze, and cannot
be replayed. No repair or outcome interpretation resets exposure or the attempt
cap. The final packet must record which mechanical retry classes are terminal
and which may be retried; this table does not itself authorize another launch.

## 6. Exposed-row policy

Choose exactly one policy, prospectively:

- **Allow exact parent reuse:** measuring the eight frozen T/H parents early may
  still satisfy that same exploratory GEO gate, provided rows, prompts, scorer,
  arms and decoding stay unchanged and no result selects a subtype, prompt,
  operator or replacement row. They are exposed and cannot become fresh
  confirmation evidence. Register this exception explicitly to original
  PREREG section 8; independent review must reconcile it with GEO's one-shot
  semantics before either freeze changes.
- **Keep screen parents unmeasured:** use an approved representative generated
  bank for predictive readiness; every measured bank row becomes exposed
  development data and is excluded from future fresh screens/confirmation.
- **Retire early-measured screen parents:** if exact parents are measured, regard
  them as spent development rows and amend GEO with independently generated
  fresh parent rows, chosen by a previously frozen rule. Preserve J hypotheses
  and membership; do not redesign from J/K outcomes. Re-freeze GEO and re-pin
  PARENTQ. This option spends membership and adds a review/budget cost.

Partial outputs expose rows too. Fix this policy before any canary or parent
generation, and record exposure by row and attempt rather than relying on a
successful terminal receipt.
