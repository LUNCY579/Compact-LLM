# Promotion policy

Only native behavior on registered held-out parents may establish efficacy.
Static hashes, synthetic tests and lower training loss do not promote a recipe.
CURRENT_BEST_RECIPE stays at the unchanged Titan with no claimed improvement until
the registered acquisition/preservation/efficiency gates pass.

After E1: acquisition+preservation -> curriculum; acquisition+forgetting -> replay/KL;
verified underfit -> data/runtime diagnosis then scope; train fit without transfer ->
fresh parents/template diagnosis. No useful online successes -> no RL.
Use matched data, tokens, scope, objective and evaluation except the single declared
factor. Independent donor competence precedes merging. Retain negative results.
