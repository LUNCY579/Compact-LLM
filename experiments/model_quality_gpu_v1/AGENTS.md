Keep this package isolated. Work on this explicitly authorized branch or a newly authorized successor. Do not edit historical RAB, CED, published source/core assets, repair registrations, sibling behavior screens, sealed banks, workflow files or other refs as a side effect.

Actual goal: useful model/system quality with explicit retention, grounding, uncertainty, format and cost tradeoffs. Synthetic tests are mechanics only. Native source qualification is not training, training completion is not improvement, and improvement on repeatedly inspected DEV is not confirmation. Do not infer capacity ceilings or equivalence from null p-values.

Use the frozen six-cell whole/response × LR design. Native v1 source is exact Supa, not Opallo. Preserve the mask, token accounting, row selection/order, update groups and objective normalization. Do not give response-only cells extra examples to match their smaller supervised-token count. No silent dataset truncation, packed-doc leakage, custom source substitution or outcome-driven threshold change.

A new qualified data plan/review and target-GPU receipt must precede native training. Never weaken source, resource, runtime or qualification checks to manufacture PASS. Never label a synthetic object as native execution. Native envelopes/checksums are integrity records, not cryptographic evidence that a model ran.

Keep raw failed attempts and create-only full training-state checkpoints. Save only at cleared-gradient update boundaries. Resume into a new attempt from an immutable COMPLETE checkpoint. Do not touch other jobs; the cooperative lock does not protect against old scripts. No automatic retries or asynchronous GPU launches.

Read PLAN/AUDIT/DESIGN/RUNBOOK and the current evidence receipt. Record any change as a new experiment or correction where warranted; do not overwrite historical results or retroactively change acceptance criteria. Native, CUDA, full historical regression and serving behavior must be independently qualified when unexecuted here.
