# Continuing toward verified quality gains

The goal remains **finding a repeatable pattern that improves Rineya**, including opposite
directions. The offline packet is preparation; its success does not complete that goal.
No native model run or quality gain has been observed. Original PARENTQ/GEO evidence, the
eight recipes, two seeds and 48 final endpoints remain unchanged.

The source now adds a guarded disposable qualification body and an own-Job guardian.
Neither imports a model at module import or accepts a fixture callback through the real
entry point. Model imports and the full checkpoint hash require a source-bound reviewed
proposal, independently bound CPU containment evidence, issued attempt/slot permit, actual
process/Job identities, retained hardware/central/temp leases and resource admission.
An issued JSON cannot authenticate a human by itself: explicit authorization in the owning
chat is separately necessary. No such authorization or permit has been provided.

The V2 specification preserves eight fixed DEV readouts, two padding checks, two disposable
optimizer updates and dense reload readout. It adds exact 1,984-slot left padding and reserves
720 seconds including bounded cleanup within the existing one-hour qualification pool.
Actual model/layer recomputation, failure requests and costs are recorded. Qualification
cannot authorize the full training sweep, use final data or establish quality. Full-program
throughput may remain inconclusive even when the small mechanics body passes.

`opposite_analysis.py` compares every fixed DEV cell without hiding failed recipes:
canonical versus diverse contracts, regular versus counterfactual examples, and zero versus
20% replay. It reports conditional reversals, interactions, retention/acquisition tradeoffs
and disagreement between seeds. Missing actual observations give **UNMEASURED**, not invented
gains. Two-seed agreement is a development hypothesis; the existing final can validate the
selected recipe, not every factorial causal claim or a universal deterministic rule.

`input_patterns.py` checks exact training doses and target-token masses without opening
final banks. Replay replaces fresh repair examples and changes the training class mixture.
Token counts alone do not establish gradient magnitude: mean target-token loss per single
example followed by accumulation gives equal example coefficients. These structural facts
generate testable opposing hypotheses and are not capability measurements.

`operating_characteristics.py` audits the predeclared decision bounds using hypothetical
loss rates. A conservative rule can be inconclusive even for a beneficial candidate, and
too few source-correct cases cannot certify retention with zero observed losses. Its
calculations do not change frozen thresholds or consume final model outcomes.

All work remains under the owned BusinessProject artifact lane. Current-source CPU receipts
and static code review must precede any proposed operator grant. Older packet snapshots remain
historical evidence for their original source hashes; additions require a new reviewed binding.
