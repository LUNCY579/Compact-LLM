# Opposite directions in Titan quality research

The strongest historical lead is retention repair by removing small update components: top 25-percent updates retained one, one and four more source answers than their paired dense controls across three independent training draws. That direction repeated; its magnitude and other quality endpoints did not. No deterministic general-quality gain with the required retention and fresh-confirmation evidence is established. The goal remains active.

## Findings that changed the next action

The completed TAPS campaign is now independently accepted as a restricted negative result. Its adaptive controller matched fixed/random validation accuracy and failed preservation, format and answerability gates. Its query-blind floor exceeded model accuracy. More recurrence or a fitted unsigned-coherence controller is not justified by that evidence.

The new Titan prototype also needed a stronger opposite-direction audit. Removing the question exposed perfect predictors in its draft corpus: the most-written register solved all 1,000 TRAIN reasoning rows, a fixed event bin solved 166/166 transitions, a unique authoritative value solved 300/300 grounding rows, and a unique timestamp value solved 168/168 retrieval rows. PILOT fact count solved 64/64 grounding rows. These are deterministic corpus shortcuts, not Rineya gains. Independent symbolic teacher agreement and85 green mechanics tests did not certify a useful learning instrument.

V1 remains unchanged and its corpus is blocked for reasoning-quality admission. The additive V2 successor generates the world before selecting opposed questions. Every parent has the same entire public input after removing only the query, the same requested format, and two distinct verified truths. A deterministic stateless query-blind answerer can score at most one of the pair. This 50-percent bound is mechanically verified over all 1,000 TRAIN and 192 PILOT parents; it is not a model result.

Visible output-format instructions are independently bound to metadata and targets. A metadata-only JSON target attached to a plain-answer prompt is rejected. Content, strict contract compliance, unsupported answers and false abstention remain separate endpoints.

## Opposite update controls

The new bounded mechanical control module specifies three transformations of the SAME future child-relative delta: dense, stable per-tensor top 25-percent, and per-tensor norm-matched uniform scaling. Scalar and random coordinate removal are different controls; a norm or support match alone cannot establish functional independence.

Apply these controls to the new joint child's update relative to its immediate Titan-Sparse parent. Subtract the parent once and add the transformed new delta once. Do not re-add the inherited Titan update, treat absolute trained tensors as deltas, re-mask the source or infer BF16 serving behavior from FP32 results. No actual model weights were transformed here.

Nine tests and ten subtests verify norms, stable ties, immutability, zero tensors and wrong-source/absolute-checkpoint rejection. They establish mechanical correctness only. A useful pruning pattern must preserve every required source-correct, reasoning, answerability and format endpoint on fresh data, compared with both dense and norm-matched alternatives.
