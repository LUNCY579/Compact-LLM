## Why this direction, and what stays closed

CC0 v1 has relevant semantic task analogues, including multi-hop links, state updates, exceptions and contradiction resolution. Different family names alone do not establish irrelevance. Its current documented repairs are broader: native-window mismatch, a strong question-only abstention cue, fixed register/form design and a substantial multi-turn mixture. Its DPO material also needs an optimization path outside the present SFT contract. The new comparison's direct audit determines exact current counts; this document does not assume that 1.7 GB is better or worse because of size, and does not substitute its old audit for fresh measurement.

The 6,000-row direction already has a complete permitted TRAIN/DEV native census, passed overall window/answer checks and a bounded list of remaining defects. That makes it the smaller repair candidate, not an accepted or scientifically superior corpus. It is primarily retrieval/abstention rehearsal; reasoning-transfer and whether training changes useful role complementarity must be measured on the unchanged MRS scout. No training benefit follows from corpus acceptance alone.
