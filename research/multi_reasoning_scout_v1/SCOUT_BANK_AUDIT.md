# MRS-1 bank audit

Classification: **OPEN / DEVELOPMENT / SELECTION-EXPOSED FOREVER**. No confirmation authority. The existing interrupted draft bank is retained byte-for-byte and verified before model observation; it is not regenerated to accommodate outcomes.

The immutable MRS-bank-1 has 120 worlds, four families of 30, 8 SMOKE + 16 CAL + 96 CHECK. It covers 3–6 register operations, counterfactual deletion, prerequisite scheduling, and three-hop evidence with superseding records. Every case has exact case/scenario IDs, public raw problem, family/split metadata, separately stored gold, answerability and difficulty. `BANK_MANIFEST.json` pins all three JSONL hashes. Deterministic seed 29170917 regenerates identical bytes; forward and separate exhaustive/backward solvers agree for every item. Additional software tests check 320 unselected random worlds.

`BANK_REAUDIT.json` verifies all 120 generative worlds remain unique after removing lexical names, not merely after assigning IDs. No derivatives cross partitions. This is world isolation, not template isolation: the small number of explicit task templates is shared by design. No generator or historical dataset row is imported. IDs have a new MRS1 namespace; there are zero collisions with quarantined IDs. `ACCESS_MANIFEST.json` contains the exact quarantine registry digest and relevant confirmation-gap, read/consumption and claim ledger metadata. Protected payloads were neither opened nor hashed. Semantic overlap with unopened protected populations cannot be certified; namespace separation is not represented as such a certificate.

Existing open alternatives were considered through their public generator/metadata contracts. RRP transition/SELECT-VIEWS instruments mainly test atomic operations; historical exposed evaluation results do not constitute a suitable multi-step, missing-evidence bank. Older residual DEV has heterogeneous formats; Architecture EAM's older bank has known answerability cues, while its later 24-world paired layout instrument targets memory readout rather than this direct-answer task. No protected or consumed confirmation bank was adopted.

The five answers 0/1/2/3/UNKNOWN each occur 24 times across the bank. CHECK counts are 17/19/20/22/18 (78 answerable, 18 unanswerable); its best constant answer scores 22/96. CAL-fitted shallow classifiers are evaluated on CHECK, not fitted there: question-only five-class accuracy is 15/96, family 14/96, length-bin 19/96, line-count 16/96, last-digit 12/96, digit-sum 17/96, and missing-marker 29/96. These are measured shortcut baselines, not independent model results. Additional binary answerability probes and balanced accuracies are recorded in `BANK_REAUDIT.json`.

Missing facts can be detected from surface clues in some families. Some missing rows are irrelevant and remain answerable; evidence-chain missingness need not contain the literal missing marker. This reduces one trivial cue but does not establish absence of shortcuts. Every model report must separate answerable/unanswerable performance and families. The bank is a bounded mechanism scout; positive results do not establish broad reasoning ability.

Role construction accepts only a typed public item and registered candidate projections. Gold, difficulty, answerability, IDs and solver results are not rendered into any role. The selector receives no correctness label. The gold file is hashed for integrity, with semantic reading confined to offline bank verification and post-generation analysis. Native generation does not import bank solvers or analysis. Tests check the information boundary and malformed-output preservation.
