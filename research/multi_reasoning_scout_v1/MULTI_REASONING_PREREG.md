# MRS-1 preregistration

Registered before any MRS-1 model result. The concrete protocol and all rendered roles are frozen by FREEZE.json. The scientific goal is to measure useful complementary correctness, not to favor multi-reasoning.

Population: 96 fixed CHECK worlds, 24/family. The 8 SMOKE and 16 CAL worlds are separate and always open development. No confirmation, ANY_READ, reserved, or consumed historical bank is an input. The seed and exact IDs are in BANK_MANIFEST.json; the bank will not be rebuilt for a future candidate. The all-bank prior is exactly 24/120 for each of five answers. CHECK priors are 17/19/20/22/18 for 0/1/2/3/UNKNOWN. The maximum constant CHECK floor is 22/96; the tested CAL-fitted missing-marker shortcut scores 29/96. An answerable-only breakdown and per-family analysis are compulsory so missing-evidence cues cannot masquerade as general reasoning.

Primary current contrast: A3 minus A2 on D1 seed 17's exact retained BF16 export. A3 minus A0 is the practical effect. A0 versus A1 examines more output allowance; A0/A1 versus A2 examines repeated attempts. Frozen Supa is a secondary mechanistic reference, not a tournament. Do not select a winning role, selector, model seed, prompt, or family after CHECK results.

Two registered diagnostic paired contrasts clarify the primary comparison: S1 minus A2 compares differentiated and repeated candidates with the same answer-only selector; A3 minus S1 measures added checker information on the same differentiated candidates. A3 minus A2 changes both candidate procedures and selector information, so it alone cannot attribute a gain solely to alternative computation. Report these diagnostics alongside the role-correctness overlaps.

The deployable result is A3/S2, never A4. A4 reports both the four-role candidate ceiling and the broader union including repeated direct and A1. S0/S1/S2 use identical candidate populations for their diagnostic comparison. Report selector correctness conditional on at least one correct candidate, misses despite a correct candidate, cases with no correct candidate, and novel correct synthesis. Independent M1 checkers cannot explicitly reject candidates they have not seen; that rejection metric is not identifiable in M1. Selecting a longer wrong answer is reported as an association, not a demonstrated verbosity mechanism.

Primary scoring is strict task-native final-answer correctness after the registered whole-JSON parser and EOS termination rule. Malformation, truncation, false abstention and unsupported answering on unanswerable cases remain separate. Report every repair and damage case, and net gain = repairs - damage. Include per-family outcomes, all six role-pair correctness contingency tables, disagreement and number of unique valid answers. Final-state/schedule correctness is independently verifiable; operation-level attribution from unstructured prose is not fabricated.

Use paired item outcomes. Exact two-sided McNemar uses the binomial tail on discordant pairs. Report a descriptive 95% percentile interval from 2,000 paired world-cluster bootstrap draws, fixed seed 1978. The current design has one item/world; future paraphrases must not be added to this frozen bank. No generated call is treated as an independent example. No significance threshold, power claim, multiplicity-adjusted confirmatory decision, or architecture-level claim is authorized by this small scout. All p-values and intervals are exploratory descriptions.

Difficulty is the generator's recorded operation/link/dependency-row count, not an empirically calibrated scale. Retain it in per-item outcomes and report difficulty and family-by-difficulty strata; constant values within some families limit interpretation. Report all 16 paired ABCD patterns and the prospective newly-useful/unnecessary/harmful scaffold masks emitted by the frozen factorial analysis.

Cost per gross repair is incremental A3-minus-A0 cost divided by repairs; cost per net recovered case divides by positive net gain and is null if net gain is zero or negative. Report tokens, calls, forward calls and seconds, plus full unique-run resource cost including smoke/CAL, diagnostics and qualification. No infinite or negative efficiency is presented as a benefit.

Prospective interpretations:

1. A3 approximately equals A2 and role errors overlap: no evidence for useful differentiated procedures in this regime. Approximate equality is descriptive; no equivalence margin/test is claimed.
2. Candidate ceiling rises but the real selector does not: generation diversity exists and selection/verification is the bottleneck.
3. A3 exceeds A2 at five logical calls each: exploratory evidence for structured reasoning diversity; unequal prompt lengths and actual compute remain limitations.
4. Many repairs and many damages: unstable additional computation; investigate gating/halting later, not unconditional use now.
5. Future C improves and B-to-D adds little: training may have absorbed scaffolded skills, subject to the full interaction analysis.
6. Future C improves and D-C exceeds B-A: descriptive complementarity/synergy.
7. Future C improves but D-C is negative: the old scaffold interferes with the new candidate.

No native/internal mechanism will be implemented from software fixtures, a blocked run, an oracle score, or a prompt-protocol gain alone. New positive evidence could motivate a later smallest bounded query refinement, sidecar checker, selector or conditional second pass, compared with query-refinement/A06/looped-flow/EAM/matched-MLP controls; it would require its own native experiment.
