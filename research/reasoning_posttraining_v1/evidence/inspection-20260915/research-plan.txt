Decision: Preserve the negative results, reject the universal capacity conclusion, and replace the old
“86% means better reasoning” premise with a controlled operator-learning experiment. Keep long-
context efficiency as a separate track. Escalate to a query-conditioned read–revise mechanism only
after a clean baseline ladder.
This is a read-only review of pinned remote sources and a prospective research design. No model
training, GPU profiling, repository modification, commit or push was performed. Source-reported tests
were not rerun. The included utility tests concern this bundle only. Sources use stable identifiers such
as [S06], resolved in the source ledger.
1. What the evidence actually establishes
1.1 The original result was not imaginary, but its interpretation was too broad
The non-recurrent branch records historical exact-match scores of 0.375 for the frozen model, 0.867 for
a trained stateless GRU control, and 0.879 for a trained depth-carried GRU, on 256 examples. Its later
audit classifies 128 wrong-to-right transitions: 104 formatting corrections, 20 abstention changes and
four content changes. These numbers are source-reported, not independently reconstructed in this
review. The rounded accuracy increase corresponds to approximately 126 net correct answers, not 128:
wins must not be confused with net wins. A complete adjudication must also account for losses. [S02]
The appropriate surviving claim is that small trained changes altered useful output behavior on that
instrument. The evidence no longer supports a roughly 49-percentage-point gain in compositional
reasoning. Four content corrections are too few, and too task-specific, to establish a general reasoning
mechanism. Conversely, formatting and abstention repair can have practical value when explicitly
evaluated as such. Do not discard a useful product improvement because it is not a new reasoning
capability.
Maintain three separate endpoints: literal output compliance, semantic correctness, and selective
answering. A model that says the correct vault with an extra carrier word should not count as a
reasoning failure. A model that gives a fluent, correctly formatted answer to the wrong question should
not count as a reasoning success. A model that abstains on an answerable problem should not
disappear from the error denominator.
1.2 A04 was misquoted in the pasted interpretation
A04 trained four arms in a 2×2 design: shared versus per-hook gates, each with and without depth-
carried state. Its reported +0.003906, p≈0.9586 is the interaction
I = (per-hook carry − per-hook stateless)
  − (shared carry − shared stateless).

It is not the trained-versus-frozen effect. There was no frozen reference arm in that factorial. Only two
items had nonzero interaction contributions, and one involved a formatting artifact. Effective gates
ended near 0.0025, about 1% of the configured maximum gate magnitude, with a failed manipulation
check. The registered classification was D: the intended gating manipulation did not meaningfully
express itself. [S06]
This rules out neither useful non-recurrent training nor a properly trained recurrent architecture. It also
does not justify automatically rerunning A04 with a larger learning rate. A05 subsequently forced
amplitudes on the already-trained adapters. On its 128-item validation bank, the approximately 0.005
amplitude performed best in the inspected grid; larger amplitudes degraded behavior and eventually
collapsed it. Forced-amplitude experiments are not equivalent to retraining, but they undercut the
simple “open the gate much more” recommendation. [S07]
There is a second important correction. A05 D3 found meaningful residual non-format error on A04’s
own evaluation bank: the P1 arm had 44 wrong abstentions and 25 wrong answers out of 256 items,
alongside 14 formatting-only errors. Thus, a format-dominated historical improvement does not imply a
format-dominated residual or an instrument with no remaining headroom. Whether a proposed
mechanism can exploit that headroom is a different question. [S07]
1.3 A09 identifies a useful behavioral failure, not a theorem about the model
A09’s carrier-normalized direct lookup accuracy was 0.895, versus 0.015 for the “immediately after”
relation under query-last. In that relational condition, 0.735 of responses were the cue’s own vault.
Counts also showed a strong near-constant wrong-answer pattern. These are consequential failures on
that checkpoint, synthetic registry and prompt family. [S05]
The behavioral description is “cue substitution”: the output resembles solving a simpler neighboring
task. It is not yet a mechanistic demonstration that an internal operator representation was erased or
never computed. Wrong binding, positional ambiguity, instruction interpretation, insufficient trained
computation or the final readout can yield similar outputs. Distinguish these with counterfactual
questions, intermediate probes and causal interventions rather than naming the cause from the answer
alone.
The short-context results in the non-recurrent report make a purely long-distance explanation less
convincing: poor composition appears on short prompts as well. However, the short and long results
come from different builders/tasks, not a matched same-world length experiment. They support “failure
persists in short contexts,” not “context length has no effect,” and certainly not “every inference
intervention has been ruled out.” [S04]
1.4 There are genuine inference-side findings worth retaining
The session report records a serving-bias candidate of 3.75 rather than the shipped approximately
5.15625. In one held-out readout comparison it reports 11 answerable gains and no losses, with
unchanged unanswerable counts. Treat this as a bounded readout-policy result, not new reasoning in
the weights and not a universal production guarantee. It is distinct from opening the sealed efficacy
gate for adapter training. [S04]
The same report says repeating the question at both ends was net harmful on its mixed bank, whereas
A09 found a real lookup benefit. Those findings can coexist: layout effects interact with task family,
instructions and baseline headroom. Neither “always duplicate the question” nor “layout never helps”
is justified. [S04, S05]

2.3 Two concrete profiler limitations require attention
At the audited commit, readout_profile/run_profile.py calls the hash-locked load_native; its --label
only names the artifact. The foreign-checkpoint support described as “building now” is not established
by that source. Keep native qualification locked. Add a separate loader contract with its own runtime,
tokenizer, dtype, cache and output-policy checks. [S08]
More importantly, outcome_at estimates the full-answer effect of each bias from the initial UNKNOWN
margin. The runner records the initial logits and a zero-bias generation, but does not record the full
sequence of competing margins. The identity
UNKNOWN wins at this decoding step iff m_this_step + bias > 0
is a per-step property, with an explicit tie convention. It does not imply that changing a bias cannot
change a later token while leaving the first unchanged. A constructive counterexample is included in 
tools/bias_trace.py. This is a generality defect in the extrapolation, not proof that every historical
estimate is wrong. The native banks may happen to satisfy stronger conditions; foreign checkpoints
must not inherit that assumption. [S08]
Conservative remedy: store complete greedy token traces. If the biased winner is unchanged at every
step, the sequence remains unchanged under the exact scalar-bias model. If any step changes,
regenerate that condition unless a separately proven terminal-abstention rule determines the whole
answer. Also qualify ties, dtype rounding, logits processors, UNKNOWN tokenization, EOS and
maximum-length truncation. Sampling needs a different analysis.
The profiler also performs an explicit uncached forward for initial logits and then calls generate with the
full input. Do not budget it as one transformer prefill without measuring whether any prefix
computation is actually reused. This is an accounting issue, not evidence of scientific invalidity. [S08]
2.4 The hybrid branch is not another Supa checkpoint result
HLGA-1 targets R2 at artifacts/rineya-core-en32k-28L: 28 layers, width 1024, 16 query heads, eight KV
heads, head dimension 128 and vocabulary 32,768. Supa-A1.1’s inspected config instead has

3. What “capacity limit” would have to mean
Several different limits are being conflated. A checkpoint may fail under a fixed direct-answer prompt. A
training recipe may fail to teach it a skill. A frozen-backbone adapter may be too restrictive to alter the
relevant computation. A deployment budget may prevent sufficient iterative work. None of these is
equivalent to showing that no model with a given number of stored parameters can represent the
required algorithm.
The user’s “DNA” analogy is useful when translated into measurable variables: the computation graph,
reusable state, trainable subspace, token representation, objective, data curriculum and available
inference computation. A better inductive bias can make a skill easier to learn or cheaper to execute. A
wrong bias can make it harder, and recovery training can consume any nominal efficiency gain.
Controlled reasoning research provides a reason not to declare a universal sub-1B ceiling. The
inspected iGSM study uses GPT2-small-scale models, synthetic dependency problems and structural
train/test separation, reporting reasoning generalization in that restricted setting. It also contrasts
depth and width. This is not proof that a lightly adapted Supa checkpoint will learn broad reasoning,
and arithmetic is deliberately simplified. It is a relevant counterweight to “only the parameter count
can explain this.” [P01, P02]
A valid operational scaling study must name its distribution and budgets. For example: “On this sealed
composition suite, within this data and optimization budget, the best qualified 0.6B system fails to
reach a predeclared threshold that a matched 1.2B system reaches.” Even that establishes a bounded
empirical frontier, not mathematical impossibility.

6. Stage 1 — build a diagnostic instrument that can falsify
the story
6.1 Generate a world first, then render it
Use a tiny executable domain-specific language describing a world, relations, state updates and a
question. Produce the answer and alternative wrong-answer categories from the world specification,
independently of the natural-language renderer. Use a separately implemented reference solver and
hand-inspected examples to avoid a generator and solver sharing the same mistaken convention.
Begin with direct retrieval, before/after relations, two-hop binding, conditionals, short state updates and
counting. Expand to deeper programs only after these primitives behave sensibly. “More examples”
from the same finite entity pool is not automatically more independent data.
For each world, create counterfactual question pairs. Keep facts fixed while changing the operator;
keep the operator fixed while changing one critical operand; reorder irrelevant facts; insert balanced
distractors; swap surface names without changing structure. Compute the cue’s own answer as a
diagnostic label, not a value exposed to the model. Exclude ambiguous neighbor boundaries or define
their semantics explicitly.
A concrete pair could use an ordered registry: A→blue, B→green, C→red. Ask for A’s vault, then for the
vault of the code immediately after A. The correct pair is blue then green. Randomize both order and
associations across instances so this cannot be solved by alphabetical position or one frequent answer.
This example is illustrative, not a proposed evaluation bank.
6.2 Separate kinds of generalization
Use instance-disjoint training/development splits for primitive skills. For composition evaluation, add
genuinely held-out program skeletons, renderer families and operator combinations. Structural
disjointness is meaningful only when the stratum has enough possible structures: a one-edge lookup
has too few skeletons to support the same split rule as a rich dependency graph. Document this rather
than making an impossible universal no-template-overlap rule.
Keep names, numerical ranges and surface syntax shifts distinct from new program structure. They test
different generalization hypotheses. Use fresh evaluation generators authored independently where
possible. A different seed for the same generator is not an independent generator; a renamed entity is
not a new reasoning structure.
Audit exact/near duplicates, world identity, structural canonicalization, answer distribution, context
length, lexical cues, position and answerability leakage. Run simple shortcut baselines: constant
answer, cue-copy, alphabetic next, last-mentioned entity, bag-of-words and a restricted token/position
classifier. High shortcut performance is a warning even when train/test text hashes differ.
6.3 Make length a matched intervention
For the same underlying problem and relevant evidence, construct short and long contexts with
controlled irrelevant content. Cross context length with operator depth and evidence location. Match
answerability and target identities. Record actual tokenizer lengths rather than word counts; define
allowed tolerance before generation. Never silently truncate the target or stretch RoPE beyond a
qualified range.

A proposed screening grid is approximately 256, 1,024 and 4,096 input tokens; later 8K/16K only when
model/runtime limits are qualified. Exact boundary tests around a sparse window are a different
mechanical suite, not a substitute for this semantic study. Primary long-context effects should be
within-world contrasts, not differences between unrelated corpora.
6.4 Score semantics without accidentally forgiving errors
Keep literal exact match, a predeclared canonical-answer parser and executor-verified correctness. The
canonical parser may remove specified carrier words when the task grammar permits; it must reject
multiple conflicting answers. Do not let arbitrary substring occurrence count as correctness. T est
leading articles, punctuation, mixed-case identifiers, answers inside negations, two candidate answers
and UNKNOWN followed by an answer.
Record wrong binding/cue substitution, wrong operation, arithmetic/state error, malformed answer,
wrong abstention and false answer on unanswerable cases. A trace is not automatically faithful just
because it sounds plausible; score any explicit intermediate fields against the world model.
Exit gate: both independent solvers agree; adversarial scorer tests pass; shortcut audits are reviewed;
a strong positive-control solver/model succeeds; the frozen target has measurable headroom;
evaluation specifications are frozen before model selection.
7. Stage 2 — cheap diagnostics before another training run
7.1 Corrected checkpoint profile
Profile the native checkpoint with its original and neutral readout policies, the shared-weight/different-
policy controls, a limited set of distinct family weight lineages, an upstream small-model control and a
larger positive control. Identify the actual models and hashes before running them; this document does
not assert that external weights are already installed.
Deduplicate storage identities but preserve intentional wrapper/precision contrasts. Use the same task
semantics and separately report each model’s native template and a controlled common template
where valid. Do not transport UNKNOWN token id 51378 or bias 5.15625 to an unrelated tokenizer.
Unknown support should be implemented and qualified per model, not assumed.
Do not interpret all-core failure as a size theorem. It is useful for selecting a stronger starting
checkpoint and discovering common failure modes. A successful family member triggers causal
ablations of precision, readout and weight lineage before attributing the result to its training data.
7.2 An intervention ladder that localizes the bottleneck
Compare direct answering with a bounded extraction-then-solve protocol. In a diagnostic-only arm,
provide gold relevant facts but not the answer. In another, provide the correct binding or intermediate
state without the final answer. In a third, ask the model to choose and execute an operation over
already supplied operands. These oracle conditions deliberately provide information; they are not
deployable improvements and must not enter the main scoreboard.
Compare model-extracted facts against oracle facts. If oracle evidence fixes the task, retrieval/binding
is a bottleneck. If correct operands still fail, execution or instruction use is implicated. If explicit
intermediate generation works but direct answer fails, investigate sequential computation and training
objectives. None of these interventions alone proves the exact internal cause, but together they
sharply reduce the hypothesis space.

8. Stage 3 — the conventional trainability ladder
8.1 Microfit is an engineering test, not a capability result
Use 32–64 training-only problems with verified labels. Require the chosen trainable path to reduce loss
and strongly fit this tiny set before scaling data. Track gradients, update norms, gate values, residual-
to-hidden ratios, logits and operator-sensitive margins. Make the microfit criterion prospective, such as
at least 95% canonical correctness on that training-only set within a bounded budget. A failed microfit
is a diagnostic failure, not proof of a 0.6B ceiling.
Avoid the dead-path initialization where both an output projection and its multiplicative gate start
exactly at zero. One safe pattern is a small nonzero output projection with an exactly zero gate: initial
behavior is preserved, the gate can receive gradient, and the inner path can learn after it opens. Verify
this in the actual runtime; algebra alone does not qualify mixed precision, hooks or caching.
The prior gate-amplitude diagnostics warn against forcing a fixed large gate because “more signal must
help.” Observe delivered perturbation and loss rather than choosing a magnitude from intuition. Gate
range, optimizer groups and clipping should be declared separately. [S06, S07]
8.2 Compare simple interventions first
Screen the frozen baseline; gate/readout-only control; existing stateless adapter; an MLP adapter; a
low-rank attention update; and, where memory permits, a full-parameter or larger-trainable-subspace
diagnostic copy. The full-update arm is a positive trainability control, not a deployment
recommendation. Use a separate checkpoint copy and never mutate the protected native reference.
The reviewed stateless GRU adds 156,225 stored parameters, but 12,288 recurrent-matrix coordinates
have no task gradient when its state is always zero. Report both stored and effective trainable
participation. Matching parameter counts does not match useful capacity or compute. The strongest
conventional baseline—not the unadapted model alone—must be the main comparator for a new
architecture. [S02]
Use one-factor objective comparisons: answer-only training; verified intermediate evidence/operator/
state supervision; and an explicit contrast against the cue-copy answer. An illustrative contrastive term
is
L = L_answer + lambda_process * L_verified_fields
  + lambda_contrast * max(0, margin − logP(correct) + logP(cue_copy)).
Use sequence log-probabilities with a declared length convention and compatible candidate formatting.
Do not optimize the gold margin on evaluation items. Only include contrastive cases whose two
answers genuinely differ. Gold intermediate fields are training labels, not inference-time inputs.

A prospective data ladder is 1K, 8K and 32K distinct training worlds, using the same mixture, split rules
and validation policy across arms. These are adjustable planning tiers, not pre-authorized runs or an
estimate of sufficiency. First screen with a small bounded budget; confirm the strongest few methods

8.3 Control recovery and forgetting
Mix a reviewed general-language/short-response preservation set into training only when its
provenance permits. Hold its weight fixed across treatments. Measure whether a method fixes operator
tasks by collapsing all output into one rigid format or by damaging ordinary dialogue, direct retrieval
and appropriate abstention. Track the complete accuracy–latency–memory tradeoff, not only the target
task.
Do not claim a general reasoning improvement from a single registry domain. Require transfer to at
least two independently rendered composition domains and one non-registry transfer domain before
broadening the language. For practical specialization, a narrower release can be legitimate if the task
contract and failure behavior are explicit.
Exit gate: a conventional method either generalizes reliably or fails in a way that a proposed
architecture specifically addresses. If ordinary low-rank or full-update training solves the clean target,
the immediate research problem becomes efficiency and retention—not inventing a mechanism to
overcome a proven capacity barrier.
9. Stage 4 — a bounded query-conditioned read–revise
mechanism
9.1 The hypothesis
The next architecture candidate should add something the tested tokenwise stateless sidecar does not
explicitly add: a small query state that re-reads token evidence after updating its representation of the
requested operation. Call this proposal QRR-1, a descriptive experiment label, not a novelty claim.
For allowed frozen token representations H, initialize state z from the actual question. At iteration t:
q_t = Wq * Norm(z_t)
K   = Wk * Norm(H)            V = Wv * Norm(H)
a_t = softmax(q_t K^T / sqrt(d) + allowed_position_mask)
r_t = a_t V
z_(t+1) = z_t + eta_t * g_t * U(z_t, r_t)
final_hidden = native_hidden + g_out * Wo * z_K
The operation must be inferred from the input question; no gold operator id or answer-derived routing
metadata is supplied at inference. A first read can identify the cue, a second can bind its neighbor, and
a later update can resolve the requested value. That is the hypothesis to test, not a behavior
guaranteed by the equations.
The one-read arm is the non-recurrent baseline. T wo or four tied updates test recurrent computation. An
untied version tests whether extra distinct parameters rather than repeated computation drive gains. A
matched MLP tests whether ordinary residual capacity suffices. A second full-transformer pass tests
whether a compact workspace is more efficient than simply doing more backbone work.

9.2 Safety and correctness of the computation
Train and test the same iteration counts initially; extrapolating to more loops is a separate experiment.
Track update norms, residual size, answer stability, oscillations, latency and evidence recall. A stable
latent fixed point is not a guarantee of a correct answer. If convergence is claimed, define the map,
norm and sufficient conditions; do not infer convergence from a decreasing arbitrary activation norm.
Causality applies during teacher forcing as well as inference. Each answer-position query may attend
only to legal earlier tokens. Do not build a memory from full target-containing sequences and
accidentally let the reader see future answers. Keep source position and iteration index distinct; adding
another RoPE clock without a clear semantic role is not a free extra computation step.
Qualify zero-gate identity, finite gradients after opening, installed/uninstalled parity, prompt batching,
padding, prefill, cached decode and serialization. If prompt H is cached, document when it is valid and
whether model-weight updates require recomputation. Count the memory for stored H/K/V and the
workspace itself; a tiny parameter count can still have a large activation footprint.
9.3 What would falsify QRR-1
If its gain disappears against a matched one-read or MLP baseline, recurrence is not credited. If the
second read attends differently but accuracy does not improve, mechanism activation is established
but utility is not. If it helps only one renderer, it is a template-specific adaptation. If it helps only with
oracle evidence, the deployable evidence selector remains the bottleneck. If it costs as much as a more
accurate larger model, the product case is weak even if the mechanism is interesting.
Universal Transformers and later latent-depth work establish related architectural precedents. They
justify considering repeated learned computation, not assuming that a tiny frozen-backbone retrofit will

12. Statistics, evidence gates and compute discipline
The unit of independence is the underlying world/problem pack, not each layout, paraphrase or length
variant. Keep all paired variants together in resampling. Model seeds add another source of variation;
do not pool repeated evaluations of the same worlds across seeds as though they were new
independent test items. The supplied comparison utility deliberately handles one declared model seed
at a time.
Predeclare the primary contrast, endpoint, test family and practically meaningful effect. For a single
independent binary pair per world, exact McNemar testing is useful. For repeated variants, report a
paired cluster bootstrap or an appropriate paired cluster randomization test. A confidence interval and
an effect size matter more than a threshold p-value. Apply a declared multiplicity method to the actual
family of confirmatory comparisons; do not choose the method after inspecting results.
Suggested research advancement criteria are: at least +5 percentage points in canonical composition
accuracy over the strongest qualified simple baseline; a confidence interval excluding zero on the
primary contrast; positive direction across three independent training seeds; transfer on two held-out
renderer/program domains; and no more than a predeclared preservation loss, provisionally 2 points.
These are proposed decision thresholds, not empirically justified universal constants or a power
guarantee. Finalize them with development variance, error costs and available sample sizes before
sealing the test.
Do not interpret p>0.05 as equivalence. T o claim practical equivalence, predeclare a margin and
require the corresponding interval to fit inside it. Zero observed harms is not proof of zero risk. For n
independent relevant opportunities and zero harms, a one-sided 95% binomial upper bound is 1 −
0.05^(1/n); correlated variants do not each count as an opportunity.
Pilot wall time on the actual hardware with warmup, synchronization, uncontended runs and the exact
precision/backend. Estimate total training and inference work from measured tokens and repeats. The
agent’s approximately 30 GPU-minute cross-core estimate is not independently verified and should not
become a commitment. Record failed runs and qualification overhead too.
A sensible budget policy releases work in increments: instrument/CPU checks; a frozen diagnostic pilot;
a small trainability screen; multi-seed confirmation; and only then architecture scaling. Do not
authorize a large native-pretraining programme from an interesting retrospective plot.
