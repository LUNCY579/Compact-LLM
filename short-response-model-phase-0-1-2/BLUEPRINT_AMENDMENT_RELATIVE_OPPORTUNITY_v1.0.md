# Blueprint Amendment — Relative Opportunity Evaluation

**Amends:** `SHORT-RESPONSE MODEL — RESEARCH AND ENGINEERING BLUEPRINT`, Version 1.0, July 2026  
**Amendment version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved

## Reason for amendment

The original blueprint correctly defines the product as a model that behaves like a capable person texting and explicitly allows a casual update to receive either an acknowledgment or one natural follow-up. It also lists initial absolute behavioral and efficiency targets. Repository experience showed that those fixed numbers could override the more important engineering question: whether a measured resource saving is worth the measured quality opportunity cost.

This amendment preserves the blueprint's core objective, phase order, sealed-evaluation discipline, human review, reproducibility requirements, and quality-before-efficiency philosophy. It changes how automated candidate promotion is calculated.

## Superseding rule

All absolute numerical promotion floors and ceilings in the original blueprint are reclassified as historical research targets or descriptive diagnostics. They do not independently accept or reject a candidate.

Official automated selection uses the policy in `RELATIVE_OPPORTUNITY_EVALUATION_POLICY.md`:

```text
candidate is proportionally worthwhile
when primary resource gain > combined proportional quality opportunity loss
```

Technical validity and required human approval remain binary prerequisites.

## Conversational-language clarification

Minor grammar, vocabulary, punctuation, slang, or word-order imperfections are acceptable when the intended meaning is understandable and functionally adequate. A natural follow-up question is allowed unless the benchmark explicitly forbids it. A single reference answer is not an exclusive answer key for open-ended conversation.

## Historical evidence

Reports created under evaluator v2 and absolute-threshold gates remain preserved as historical evidence. They must be rescored under evaluator v3 before their automated decisions are used by a later phase.
