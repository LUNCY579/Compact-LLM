# Claim Rules

## Allowed after development only

- A projection or merge was structurally viable.
- A candidate improved the frozen development objective.
- Delta agreement or layer effects were observed descriptively.
- A method was promoted by the preregistered funnel.

Every such statement must say `development-selected` and report preservation
results, candidate count, and the full relevant comparison curve.

## Allowed after one fresh sealed confirmation

- The frozen candidate improved the specified sealed objective relative to the
  frozen Supa baseline, with effect size and cluster-aware uncertainty.
- The output retained the target parameter count and donor-free inference.
- A predeclared directional comparison replicated.

## Not allowed from this study alone

- The 0.6B model has 4B/8B inherent capacity or is equivalent to an 8B model.
- A particular layer or head uniquely contains reasoning.
- A development optimum is universal.
- A non-significant point estimate is a confirmed capability gain.
- A gain caused by a logit offset, storage dtype, tokenizer drift, or evaluator
  change is evidence for parameter transfer.
- Repeated sealed access remains confirmatory.

## Negative-result language

If no candidate passes Merge GO, report that the tested projection assumptions
did not yield a preserved improvement. Do not generalize to all possible
cross-scale transfer. Preserve the failure boundary, projection damage, and
coefficient curves.
