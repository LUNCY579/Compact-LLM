# Conceptual Inspirations

No examples from the works below are included in the dataset. They informed the choice of reasoning capabilities and evaluation safeguards.

- Sileo, D. and Lernould, A. (2023). *MindGames: Targeting Theory of Mind in Large Language Models with Dynamic Epistemic Modal Logic*. Findings of EMNLP 2023.
- Lin, B. Y. et al. (2025). *ZebraLogic: On the Scaling Limits of LLMs for Logical Reasoning*. ICML 2025.
- Srivastava, A. et al. (2022). *Beyond the Imitation Game: Quantifying and Extrapolating the Capabilities of Language Models*. BIG-Bench.
- Suzgun, M. et al. (2022). *Challenging BIG-Bench Tasks and Whether Chain-of-Thought Can Solve Them*. BIG-Bench Hard.
- Kazemi, M. et al. (2025). *BIG-Bench Extra Hard*.
- Tafjord, O., Dalvi, B., and Clark, P. (2021). *ProofWriter: Generating Implications, Proofs, and Abductive Statements over Natural Language*.
