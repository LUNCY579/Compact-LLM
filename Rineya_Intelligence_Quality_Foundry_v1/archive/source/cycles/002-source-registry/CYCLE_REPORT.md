## Next mechanism design

The reviewed blueprint proposes 54 fresh-world attempt slots: three mechanisms,
three genuinely different inference structures, two reasoning levels and three
case configurations. Planned roles are 36 TRAIN and 18 exposed DEV. Counts are
intentions, not constructed or verified independent worlds. Failed attempts and
derivatives must retain their true lineage rather than inflate a denominator.

Determinate rows must pass actual answer-sensitive source/rule interventions,
distributed-document/position gates and causal checks for the distinguishing
structural feature. Status-only controls cannot masquerade as deep contextual
reasoning. Paired length views preserve all semantic atoms and only change
