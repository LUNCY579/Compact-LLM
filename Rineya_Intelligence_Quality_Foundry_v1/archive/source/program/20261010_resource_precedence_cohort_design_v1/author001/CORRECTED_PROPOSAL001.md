## Formal task semantics

A finite action universe has at most eight distinct ASCII action IDs, each at most eight bytes, and at most eight similarly bounded goal IDs. IDs remain identical across all within-parent task, catalog, query, and operator alternatives. A plan is a trace of distinct action IDs accepted by the selected AND/OR prerequisite net. The selected catalog assigns each action a nonnegative integer guard vector g, consumption c, production p, duration d, charge k, operator type, and goal effects. Normative integer bounds are g,c,p in 0..6 per coordinate; d in 1..4; k in 0..31; goal utility in 1..15; query deadline in 1..32. Resource states are r in {0,...,12}².

For action a, require r≥g(a) and r-c(a)≥0. Form u=r-c(a)+p(a), then apply its selected operator. Operator T_b maps u to (u0-b,u1+b); operator B_b maps u to (u0,u1-b), where b in {0,1}. Every intermediate and final resource vector must remain within {0,...,12}². A violated guard, negative stock, or upper-bound overflow makes the trace infeasible. Each active precedence edge between selected actions imposes their trace order; unselected actions do not create obligations. Elapsed time is the sum of durations, monetary cost the sum of charges, and query utility the sum of completed permitted-goal utilities. The trace must satisfy the deadline, its selected prerequisite net, and at least one permitted terminal goal. There is no parallel execution, repeated action, implicit reset, or undeclared replenishment.

Operator hypotheses also have a declared marker projection z?z XOR b. A probe episode separately records marker input and observed marker output; its normative probe interface establishes legality for both resource hypotheses without exposing the resource vector. These observations identify b=input XOR output. Learned transfer/burn behavior must later affect an actual resource-bearing call; marker differences alone fail. The initial proposal's unverifiable requirement that an unobserved probe resource state differ from the plan state is removed. No concrete probe, resource state, or world is supplied here.

Optimize feasible traces by maximizing utility, then minimizing elapsed duration, then monetary cost. Lexicographic trace order is only canonical serialization. Define Dcore(w)=(kind,sorted completed-goal IDs,sorted selected action IDs), where kind is PLAN or INFEASIBLE. Dcore excludes order, elapsed duration, utility, cost, and ending resource. Define Q(w)=(Dcore,canonical trace,utility,elapsed,cost,ending resource). Every parent and every required counterfactual must have a unique optimal Dcore before the final lexicographic tie-break; otherwise its sensitivity certificate fails. An order-only, timing-only, cost/end-only, ID-only, or lexicographic-only change cannot satisfy a decision gate. Changing selected alternatives or feasibility is mandatory here. No feasible trace yields canonical INFEASIBLE.
