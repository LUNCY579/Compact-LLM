# Evidence, independent quality review and retention design

30 September 2026, America/Chicago. This is a proposed protocol and a primary-source
reading record. It does not amend the active centralized experiment, authorize
training, establish model gains, or turn the exposed pilot into sealed evaluation.

The centralized report binds the 596,049,920-parameter Titan devfreeze and a
qualified short CE512 mechanics scope. Capturing scored responses, sustained E1
training, long-context runtime and capability preservation remain separate gates.
The model's declared 40,960-token context is not a qualified training/evaluation
envelope. Preserve the existing experiment's TRAIN allocations and closed
CHECK/FINAL banks. New data and longer scopes require a new prospective contract.

## What research supports, and what remains a hypothesis

| Primary source | Supported design implication | Limit for this project |
|---|---|---|
| [IFEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07911) and [official code](https://github.com/google-research/google-research/tree/master/instruction_following_eval) | Explicit instructions can have independently executable checks. Score each requested constraint and the all-constraints conjunction. | Format checks do not establish factual truth, useful reasoning or natural-language breadth. Adapt the method to original prompts rather than importing benchmark instances into training. |
| [AutoIF](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13542) | Instruction/data construction can use execution feedback and tests of the verification code. | If one author writes task, oracle, checker and tests, shared errors can self-certify. Independent public reconstruction, adversarial negatives and reviewer calibration remain necessary. |
| [Let's Verify Step by Step](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.20050) | Intermediate reasoning steps can receive correctness supervision; final-answer correctness alone can hide bad reasoning. | Reported mathematical-task benefits do not prove universal transfer or an optimal trace length for Titan. Use independently replayable states/steps and requested concise derivations; avoid treating plausible prose as verified process labels. |
| [Continual-T0](https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.410/) | Rehearsal is a concrete continual-learning baseline alongside measurement of old and new tasks. | Its model/task results cannot establish a universal replay ratio, no-forgetting guarantee or Titan dose. |
| [Judging LLM-as-a-Judge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685) | Model judgments have position, verbosity, self-preference and reasoning limitations. Blind candidate identity, require evidence, and calibrate on trusted controls. | Several agents with the same inherited model are procedural separation, not independent error sources or calibrated human annotators. Agreement is insufficient evidence of correctness. |
| [Proving Test Set Contamination](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17623) | Contamination tests can provide evidence under specific exchangeability/memorized-order assumptions. | A negative result does not prove every example is unseen, catch every paraphrase, or establish complete pretraining exclusion. Record the tested scope and unknown history. |
| [The Curse of Recursion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.17493) and [Accumulating Real and Synthetic Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01413) | Recursive replacement can lose distributional support; retaining original data changes that behavior in the studied settings. Preserve immutable anchors and provenance and track rare skills/diversity. | These findings concern particular recursive-data mechanisms. They do not say every synthetic row causes collapse or that merely accumulating unlimited rows guarantees continual skill retention. |
| [LongBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.14508) and [LongBench v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.15204) | Long-context assessment needs multiple tasks, including understanding and reasoning, beyond single-fact retrieval. | Public benchmark scores and out-of-scope JSON distractor pilots do not establish natural-document comprehension, evidence integration or train-long capability. |

## Every-row quality workflow

1. Register capability targets, source rights, canonical parent identity, source
   revision, role and derivative ancestry before solving, variants, teacher calls
   or difficulty estimation. TRAIN derivatives cannot enter DEV/CAL/CHECK/FINAL.
   Compare exact normalized content, source documents, tokenized prompts, semantic
   worlds with names/IDs removed, and graph isomorphism where feasible. Hashes
   establish byte identity, not authenticated execution or complete novelty.
2. A generator proposes public messages and a candidate target. A separate
   interpreter reconstructs the full public specification and trusted framing,
   without importing the generator solver or reading a hidden construction
   witness. Private metadata must join to public content; disagreement quarantines
   the row. Mathematical impossibility/UNKNOWN needs a proof under public rules.
3. Execute applicable strict parsing, type, correctness, process-replay and output
   contract checks. Run relevant-evidence changes, archive/order changes,
   unauthorized-content changes and shortcut controls. An inapplicable test has a
   null outcome and contributes no pass. Keep failed attempts and actual counts.
4. Review every row using separate semantic and adversarial assignments. Reviewers
   receive public messages and the evidence needed to judge them. Initial solving
   is blind to the proposed target and other reviewer opinions. Require an exact
   row/packet binding, independent answer, actual evidence spans or finite-state
   witnesses, disposition and limitations. Different task IDs alone do not prove
   independent models, qualified annotation or fresh-context blinding.
5. A coordinator compares independent results against the validated task contract.
   Disagreement, missing evidence, ambiguity, unsupported claims, wrong steps,
   target-format violations, leakage, or an invalid verifier cause quarantine.
   Semantic judgments outside executable contracts require stronger calibrated
   review. Do not resolve a correctness dispute by majority vote alone.
6. Repairs create a new row revision. Changed row bytes invalidate old review
   receipts, token profiles and release bindings. Re-solve and re-review the entire
   revised row. Bounded repair attempts end in quarantine if unresolved; repeated
   leniency must not replace acceptance criteria.
7. Validate every compiled example with the exact tokenizer/template, completion
   mask, EOS handling, input/target budgets and consumer interface. Never truncate
   a verified target or silently shorten a long task to count it as usable.
   Actual row fit and native training eligibility are different certifications.
8. Publish an immutable version only after complete row accounting and all required
   evidence are present. Samples can audit the reviewers' quality and estimate
   false acceptance; they cannot replace the requested full row coverage.

Row review is evidence coverage, not proof of zero residual error. Preserve
gold-control families with known mistakes, low-information/ambiguous tasks and
correct difficult cases. Freeze their interpretation before calibrating reviewers.
Measure false acceptance, false rejection, evidence completeness and disagreement
by defect and family. A changed rubric/model/checker must requalify on fresh
controls and re-review affected rows; never optimize to a permanently exposed
judge-calibration bank alone.

Defect codes should separate answer/process correctness; grounding/evidence;
ambiguity or conflicting requirements; instruction priority/output contract;
rendering/public-private mismatch; tokenizer/mask/EOS/truncation; provenance or
source rights; exact/semantic leakage; shortcut/difficulty artifacts; diversity
collapse; review identity/evidence gaps; and unsupported quality/model claims.
Correctness, ambiguity, leakage and review-integrity defects are release blockers.
Fluency scores cannot compensate for these blockers.

## Receipt integrity and independent evidence

Bind a review to the exact row bytes and assigned packet, reviewer/role,
model/deployment identity when available, rubric/prompt versions, actual independent
answer and concrete findings. Preserve the review-code source, execution result and
request/output records. Check assignments and coverage rather than trusting a
row's self-declared `reviewed=true`. Check generator/reviewer actor aliases before
allowing non-author acceptance. A receipt hash alone does not prove a model read
the row, a provider call happened, or the claimed source created the receipt.

This pilot's adversarial review uses a public-only independent interpreter and
agent inspection of compact evidence/outcome traces, with every record parsed.
The reviewer had prior exposure to verifier/generator source and aggregate
mechanical outcomes; its context is not fully independent of authoring conventions.
Neither that review nor repeated same-model agreement is calibrated human
full-prose annotation. The explicit out-of-scope archive design is a structured
context smoke test, not representative natural-document comprehension.

## Retention and model promotion

Build protected panels before training: old reasoning, instruction following,
retrieval/evidence attribution, abstention, language/conversation, short and long
context, and each newly acquired skill. Include source-correct membership from
actual qualified baseline responses, with prompt/model/tokenizer/decoding/scorer
identities frozen. Enroll newly acquired A before teaching B; original-source
enrollment alone cannot measure whether the newly acquired A survived.

Use canonical parents as the independent units. Variants, counterfactual twins,
multiple decodes and the same world scored across seeds are correlated. Bootstrap
whole parents within family strata, keeping children and corresponding before/after
responses together. Preserve per-seed/per-family outcomes. Three training seeds
are a useful replication screen, not strong characterization of the entire
training-seed population. A failing seed remains visible.

For each protected family j prospectively fix:

- a noninferiority margin delta_j on parent-level correctness change, and require
  its one-sided simultaneous lower confidence bound to exceed -delta_j;
- a loss margin rho_j on source-correct parent loss, and require its one-sided
  simultaneous upper bound to lie below rho_j; report source-incorrect repairs
  separately, because repairs cannot cancel lost mastered answers;
- output failure margins, task-specific positive controls and qualified context
  bands. A missing panel is UNMEASURED, and zero source-correct membership gives an
  undefined loss rate rather than perfect retention.

Choose the confidence procedure and multiplicity policy before final candidate
responses. Conservative Bonferroni allocation or a prospectively qualified
simultaneous cluster procedure is possible. Freeze the candidate before sealed
confirmation; DEV selects, CHECK compares fixed endpoints, FINAL confirms under
a separate power/confirmation plan. Do not change a margin after seeing a loss.
The centralized protocol's existing 3pp/2pp/5pp DEV screens are exploratory and
must not be retrospectively described as a noninferiority certificate.

For an illustrative independent binary parent-loss endpoint with zero observed
losses among m pre-enrolled mastered parents, the exact one-sided upper bound is
`1 - alpha**(1/m)`, obtained by solving `(1-q)**m = alpha`. At alpha=0.05, 299
independent mastered parents are needed to put this bound below 1%. Across eight
Bonferroni panels with alpha_j=0.05/8, that illustrative count is 505 per panel.
Those counts do not apply to correlated child rows, all seed populations, or an
arbitrary weighted correctness endpoint. Use the qualified baseline mastery rate
and expected discordance in a prospective power design; do not assert a final
sample size by counting every sibling row as independent.

Only actual held-out model behavior can establish intelligence acquisition and
preservation. Dataset-mechanics/review/tokenizer gates, low training loss, source
hashes and invented model-success rates cannot promote a model recipe. Keep
DATA_QUALIFIED, TOKENIZER_BOUND, NATIVE_ELIGIBLE and MODEL_EFFICACY_CONFIRMED as
distinct states, with every unknown explicitly recorded.

## Adaptive mixtures and continuous improvement

Keep a permanent trusted anchor/replay reservoir per protected capability and
separate pools for new-skill acquisition, compositional bridges, long-context
evidence reasoning, answerability, and language/instruction behavior. Replay
parents/sources are disjoint from evaluation; replay may resemble protected skills
without rehearsing protected evaluation instances. Preserve special exclusions
already frozen in existing experiments. Do not infer a universal requirement to
exclude entire skill families from all future replay.

Allocate by actual processed and supervised tokens, not raw rows. Track active
evidence density, target supervision density, positions, hops, distractor types,
answerability and representation diversity. Short-answer long-input tasks may
need deliberate loss/dose design; demanding a universal minimum supervision
fraction can remove the very long-context tasks being studied.

Use only qualified TRAIN_SELECTION/DEV error taxonomies to propose a new fixed
mixture. Adjust one declared factor at a time, with matched total token/update
budgets, and then freeze the revision. A family nearing its retention margin
earns more family-relevant replay or a smaller acquisition dose; scarce high-value
skills keep explicit minimum budgets. If these budgets cannot all fit, diagnose
the constraint instead of pretending equal small slices protect everything.
Historical Rineya evidence suggests nonlinear replay protection and harmful
overdosing in some settings; 50/50 and atomic targets are candidates from specific
experiments, not universally optimal rules.

The self-improvement loop is: detect a documented defect or qualified DEV failure;
identify the missing primitive/context condition; construct new original TRAIN
parents and fresh held-out diagnostic parents under separate governance; run all
row gates and reviewer calibration; freeze a new release/controlled arm; measure
acquisition and each protected capability; retain both positive and negative
results. Add richer natural-document evidence integration and new skills as
qualified scopes grow. Keep the stable anchors and rare cases rather than
replacing them with the latest generator's easiest successful outputs.
