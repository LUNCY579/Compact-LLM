# Rineya intelligence dataset program

Plan RIQF-001, 30 September 2026, America/Chicago. Quality takes precedence over
volume. This is a separate dataset research program; it does not amend the frozen
central experiment or authorize training.

## Goal and present evidence

Acquire transferable new skills, improve use of long contexts and precise output
instructions, and preserve demonstrated existing skills. Each claim needs its own
behavioral evidence. Dataset correctness, more examples, agreement between agents,
and lower training loss do not establish intelligence gain or lack of forgetting.

The supplied CENTRALIZED_EXPERIMENT_REPORT.pdf has eight pages. Its snapshot and
the associated protocol hashes are in `evidence/source_snapshot.json`. The current
student is dense BF16 Titan devfreeze, 596,049,920 parameters. Declared context
40,960 is unqualified. Short unpadded completion-only CE512 mechanics passed; the
current 5,120 TRAIN rows have maximum total length 398. Sustained training, current
capture/decoding, long-context preservation, calibrated language grading,
acquisition and retention remain unqualified or unmeasured. Current best remains
unchanged Titan. Later changes by the branch owner require a new read-only snapshot.

All new work stays inside this foundry. Existing datasets, source, reports, queue,
checkpoints and scientific ledgers stay under their current owners. Do not launch
a competing model worker, change their Python environments, or read reserved
CHECK/FINAL payloads. No existing evaluation row is recycled into a new training
example. No paid teacher or cloud work is part of this program.

## What constitutes a useful dataset

Every accepted example must have a clear training purpose, an answer justified by
what the student sees, and a verifier or explicit reference-review procedure.
Measure six separate axes: correctness, grounding, instruction adherence,
reasoning dependence, diversity/transfer, and training integration. Failure in one
axis is not offset by high scores elsewhere. Coverage is counted in independent
parents and structural families as well as rendered rows and tokens.

Start with original, objectively gradable tasks. Expand into realistic licensed
documents only after source intake and claim verification work reliably. Treat
public research benchmarks as excluded evaluation material. Research mechanisms
may inspire original tasks; copying their questions/answers does not create a
clean training corpus.

Stages 0/9/10 are coordination specifications for the experiment owner. This
foundry implements dataset-side preparation only. The 72-row pilot exercises
stages 2-8 at research-pilot status; it does not satisfy every production gate.

## Capability curriculum

The full program covers numerical and symbolic reasoning; state updates;
reference resolution; planning and constraints; precise output; grounded
answerability; dialogue corrections; contextual rules/DSL learning; source
synthesis; code/repository comprehension; safe tool-use contracts; multilingual
behavior where qualified; and realistic long-context use. See
`LONG_CONTEXT_CURRICULUM.md` for task and evidence-position controls.

Separate length, reasoning depth, necessary-record count, distractor similarity,
evidence distance, authority conflicts, new rules and output complexity. Increase
one axis at a time before combined stress. A long prompt with a short lookup is
a useful control, not broad long-context reasoning data.

Proposed training ladder: qualified <=512 first; future 1024, 2048, 4096, 8192,
16384 and 32768 total-token profiles only after their runtime and behavior are
qualified. Budget target plus EOS inside the ceiling. Near-40,960 work requires
separate qualification. No declaration or tokenizer measurement grants native
model capacity. Preserve short rows throughout longer-context curricula.

Within every length profile mix retrieval, distributed multi-hop joins,
many-record aggregation, chronological updates, policy exceptions, insufficient
evidence and concise synthesis. Record how many facts actually determine the
answer. Use necessary-evidence ablation, active-fact counterfactuals, irrelevant
record invariance and evidence position siblings. Keep all siblings with their
parent and do not inflate effective sample size.

The first pilot has six deterministic structured families: authoritative state,
relational aggregation, precedence planning, finite contextual rule induction,
instruction hierarchy and answerability. These train/test the quality machinery.
Its six shared writing/algorithm templates do not constitute unseen-template
transfer or realistic document diversity. CAL/DEV are exposed development data.

## Source registry and historical intake

For each source record path/URL, owner, revision/date, file hash, actual licence
text, permissions, permitted use, source role, exclusions and retention policy.
Original synthetic records have generated-world origin rather than scraped text;
their intended licence does not resolve rights in imported assets.

Do not trust an origin-version string when source-file membership contradicts it.
Existing language lineage has a documented 4,000-row version-label defect; recover
origin using verified original files. Public aliases/rewordings do not break
ancestry. Existing TRAIN can supply reviewed replay; it cannot become fresh held
out confirmation through a new split.

Inventory existing TRAIN sources and authorized exclusion-hash manifests without
opening closed banks. For legacy corpora, inspect a bounded TRAIN pilot, resolve
source/licence/schema issues, then validate every admitted row. Extend source
fingerprints to documents, revision series, conversational threads, repository
families and generated semantic graphs. Exact, normalized and token hashes are
different tests; none alone proves semantic or pretraining exclusion.

The first pilot uses a new namespace and no copied corpus rows, but broad
historical semantic overlap is NOT_AUDITED. That remains a production admission
blocker, not a false zero-overlap claim.

## Split firewall and diversity

Reserve parents before answers/difficulty are computed. Use an immutable split
registry across batches: canonical semantic-parent digest, source family,
structural template ID, all derivative edges, and assigned role. A new seed or
entity name does not create a new structure. Isomorphic tasks share a structural
cluster even when their values differ.

TRAIN and TRAIN_SELECTION guide fitting/selection. CAL estimates grader quality.
DEV supports exploratory recipe development. Existing CHECK/FINAL remain closed;
future confirmation banks are constructed by a separate custodian after the
candidate and confirmation policy are frozen. A closed payload inspected by a
generator or reviewer becomes exposed and must be labeled accordingly.

Reserve transfer slices for unfamiliar wording, source domains, compositions,
logic structures, rules and lengths. Distinguish unseen instance from unseen
structure and unseen domain. Ensure positive/negative answerability, benign and
hostile hierarchy controls, balanced answer positions, entity aliases and answer
frequencies. Do not enforce artificial perfect balancing when it distorts
realistic distributions; record controlled vs natural distributions separately.

## Reasoning and outputs

Prefer short verifiable final answers for deterministic bootstrap tasks. Store
audited evidence/derivations privately for review. Do not teach fabricated long
reasoning or treat a fluent explanation as proof. Explanatory SFT is a separate
registered intervention with step correctness and answer consistency checks.

For natural language targets, keep a claim ledger: each statement's source span,
confidence/qualifier, contradiction handling and omission rationale. Reference
answers may be sets/rubrics rather than one string. Every final answer must obey
the requested language, format, scope and brevity. Exact JSON checks cannot judge
prose adequacy; model judges require independent reference calibration and
adjudication, and same-model consensus is weak evidence.

For code, use executable fixtures in an appropriate sandbox with bounded
resources. For tools, validate typed arguments against a frozen tool schema and
simulate results; never execute arbitrary generated commands during dataset QA.
For preferences/DPO, pairs must share the prompt, both answers must be separately
checked, and the reason for ranking must be recorded. For RL, reward-verifier
qualification, policy sampling and likelihood/capture gates are additional work;
an oracle transcript is not an on-policy success.

## Retention and training mix

Create disjoint replay TRAIN and preservation guards for every protected
capability. Record baseline competence from actual unchanged-Titan responses
under the identical qualified tokenizer/runtime/decoding. A guard becomes
source-correct only after those responses pass its qualified scorer. Historical
claims or expected answers cannot enroll baseline membership.

Start a future registered comparison with a conservative mixture, for example
50% acquisition/40% broad replay/10% composition by nonpadding tokens. This is a
proposal for an isolated future experiment, not a universal optimum and not a
change to current E1's frozen 16 acquisition +4 replay per20 sequence schedule.
Record sequence and supervised-token exposure too: token and sequence weighting
can produce different doses. Use per-sequence completion means consistently with
the active experiment or register any changed loss normalization.

Tune replay only with allowed TRAIN_SELECTION/DEV evidence. If a protected family
loses competence, adjust its training-safe replay dose or the curriculum as one
matched factor; do not copy failed evaluation rows. Rehearse both old skills and
relevant compositions. Acquisition gains and source-incorrect repairs cannot
cancel source-correct losses. Retain a floor for all protected families rather
than redirect all replay toward one currently failing metric.

Measure base, short SFT control, proposed curriculum and replay ablation with
matched parents, update scope, dose, tokens and decoding. Learning rate,
architecture, data, length and objective must not change simultaneously. Native
integration and experimental design belong to the active experiment owner.

## Behavioral promotion and uncertainty

Report acquisition effect per family and seed; output/instruction failures;
source-correct losses; source-incorrect repairs; long-context evidence-position
and length effects; and efficiency/cost. Evaluate at frozen endpoints and include
all attempted failures. Resample entire parents within family strata; variants
and seed views stay together. Do not pool row-by-seed observations as independent.

Follow current central screens exactly when participating in that study: DEV
gain >=3pp, aggregate preservation loss <=2pp, represented-family loss <=5pp,
malformed/EOS increase <=1pp; three-seed pilot intervals are exploratory screens,
not proof of noninferiority. This foundry does not relax or replace them.

A future confirmation design must prospectively define protected panels,
noninferiority/loss margins, multiplicity and power. Require each panel's lower
confidence bound on accuracy delta to exceed its negative margin and upper bound
on source-correct loss to stay below its loss margin. Zero losses among m genuinely
independent mastered parents give one-sided95% upper bound1-0.05**(1/m): at least299
are needed to get below1%, before multiplicity. With eight Bonferroni panels the
corresponding zero-loss count is505 per panel. These counts do not solve
dependence, representative coverage or seed generalization. Undefined
denominators remain undefined. A pilot of72 rows cannot certify retention.

## Continuous self-improvement

The supervisor prioritizes confirmed defects, verifier weakness, provenance,
diversity and meaningful evidence horizon before volume. One bounded cycle:

1. Read state and immutable receipts; acquire the dataset-cycle lock.
2. Classify current defects without reading sealed evaluation material.
3. Assign a planner and a generator with explicit owned paths and acceptance criteria.
4. Require a separate verifier implementation and blind public-prompt solver.
5. Generate at most72 fresh parents initially; every attempt enters the ledger.
6. Audit each row, create blind packets, and assign semantic/adversarial agents.
7. Quarantine disputed parents and all their variants; repair at most three times.
8. Repeat all affected checks and review after any change, under a new version.
9. Freeze a research release only after exhaustive coverage; preserve negative results.
10. Improve one measured weakness in the generator/verifier, then repeat.

Row-count targets never justify dropping hard failures from reports or replacing
them silently. Failed/partial directories remain evidence. Whole batches and
manifests are write-once. Reviewers cannot release their own generation. The
release controller verifies exact counts, hashes, assignments and unanswered
findings; it does not treat a self-reported PASS as adequate evidence.

After the first cycle, scale only after a new mechanism or structural-diversity
audit passes. Do not generate more of the same six templates merely because all
72 examples pass. Prefer new reasoning structures, realistic sources with known
rights, and better calibrated review. Continue across scheduled chat wakeups
with bounded batches and quiet notifications while nothing material changes.

## Deliverables and milestones

M0: source snapshot and read-only integration boundary. M1: executable quality
pipeline with seeded failures. M2: exhaustive reviewed/tokenized72-row pilot.
M3: historical TRAIN-source intake and overlap registry. M4: source-grounded and
structurally diverse TRAIN pilot. M5: native qualified short/long release and
baseline preservation enrollment. M6: matched student acquisition/retention
experiment. M7: frozen independent confirmation. M0-M2 are dataset preparation;
M5-M7 require real student behavior and coordination with the experiment owner.

Success means improved held-out behavior with acceptable per-family retention,
not production of a large synthetic archive. If data quality passes but learning
does not, report the negative result and diagnose task learnability, transfer,
runtime and training dose before adding volume.
